The task the accepted emit record hands on after its contract: "The wiring
task emits one shape, and `settling::SetBy` already carries its three ways, so
crossing it is a translation rather than a decision." M4-35 settles what a
concept comes to, M4-36 which periods a filer has, M4-37 the surface both are
written into; this puts the three together for one filer.

The claims pinned above are the record's, re-derived on `71365cc`. Since then
the registry's mapping has not moved and the kinds of CIK 0002003750 and CIK
0001859199 are unchanged, so they should hold. The record's own claims for the
dividend-suspension fixture are not pinned: M4-33 made that filer `bank` after
they were derived, and they wait on a human. The `NotApplicable` claim above
comes from the published clause, not from those claims.

Not in this diff: `normalize()` and the golden harness moving onto this, and
the normalize golden fixtures, which fix the rendering the record leaves to
them. The benchmark gate's proof of catch slows the first one-line `pub fn` in
`lib.rs`, which is `normalize()` today, and it has to stay the stage that the
benchmark measures.
