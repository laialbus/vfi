GOALS.md's fifth M5 criterion names "dividend-based valuation" last among
the dividend metrics, and every record around it is accepted while none
forms it: the catalogue names its input and its consumer, answer 6 fixes
the one constraint on how it reads a series, the growth rate record states
what the valuation will find in its rate and leaves whether to read it to
this record, the discounted cash flow record fixes a margin's form and
names this valuation's margin as its pair, and the share-basis record
settles the comparisons that margin needs. The form itself, its base
dividend, its settings and their presets have no record.

Why a record, and not the metric's task. The valuation's free parameters,
a required return and a growth rate, are each an assumption anchor 5 says
must state its reason; whether the growth is a setting or the computed rate
is a choice the growth rate record names and declines to make; and the
base dividend under answer 6 is a reading of a series that a worker must
not guess. One record settles these with their reasons, as the discounted
cash flow record settled its own.

Why now. The last plan held this record because its margin would mirror
the one the share-basis record was to settle, and that record is on main.
Two open texts stating the form were opened in this run, and the page that
publishes a cost of equity opened whole on two fetches.

It depends on nothing because everything it reads is on main. It owns one
new file, so it runs beside M5-54 and M5-55 on `crates/analyze/` and
beside M5-57 on the ADR README. The metrics are queued under the record's
acceptance, not here.

If the record finds the derivation cannot be stated without an operation
outside the arithmetic record's table, a new reason, a change to a frozen
contract's bytes, or a v3 concept, it says so by name as the acceptance
above provides. If it cannot be written at all without an accepted record
changing, that is an escalation.
