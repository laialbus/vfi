GOALS.md's second M5 criterion, that valuing a shortlist fits inside the
free tier's daily allowance. `docs/adr/price-provider.md` is accepted, and it
makes the record of what was spent an explicit input and output of the
provider. It then says where that record is kept between sessions "is store's
(M6), and is not decided here". Its Decision review does not accept that
answer. It says the spent record has no home between sessions, that until it
has one a restart forgets the counts and the pace and the limits hold only by
the source refusing, and that `filer-decision-ledger.md` rejected store
implementing what fetch is handed. It leaves the question to a later record.
This is that record.

A kept record is a storage schema and its way in is an interface, and either
alone is above a worker. The task that implements the provider waits on this
record's acceptance, and on M5-06's.

`price-provider.md` leaves the question open by name, so this record
supersedes nothing in it. If the work finds it cannot be done without
changing something that record decided, that is an escalation.
