  - 'Given a concept, one rule eligible for it, one period and the facts of one
    filing, `vfi-normalize` answers which of those facts answer that period for
    that rule — one fact for a `tag`, one per element operand for a `sum`, and
    one for the element term of a `difference` — or which operand nothing
    answered.'
  - A flow concept is answered only by a duration fact and a balance concept only
    by an instant fact, each as the published vocabulary measures it. Neither is
    built from the other — two instants are not averaged and a duration is not
    read at its end date — and a test states both refusals.
  - The dates are compared for equality exactly as the published fetch surface
    carries them. A duration answers when its start and its end are the period's;
    an instant answers when it is the period's end. There is no day count, no
    window and no tolerance anywhere in the answer.
  - Every element operand of a `sum` or a `difference` is read at that same one
    period, so no composition mixes a quarter with a year to date. An operand
    nothing answers is reported as the operand it is; it is never read as zero
    and never dropped.
  - 'An entry whose `answers` field names the filing it was reported in is
    answered by its instant fact in that filing rather than at the period''s end.
    Where the field takes its default, the period asked for is the period
    tested. Both readings are exercised.'
  - A fact answers only in the unit the published vocabulary names for the
    concept. Where more than one fact under one entry answers one period, the
    answer names every one of them rather than choosing between them.
  - No fifth rule form appears. A duration the filing does not carry is not built
    by subtracting one duration from another, and nothing here composes a period
    the filing did not report.
  - The merged fetch fixture is what this is checked against rather than facts
    written to suit it. Its filer carries durations of ten different lengths, one
    of its filings carries three durations of one element at once, and it carries
    a cover-dated share count in every filing and never at a period end — each of
    those is a case here.
  - 'Two questions are not answered here and no answer to either is written:
    whether a `difference`''s concept operand resolved to a `Value`, which only a
    resolved concept can say, and which of several answering rules wins. Nothing
    ranks, nothing is dropped for its `reading`, and the four steps are not
    written.'
  - Nothing resolves. No filing is read by the stage, the stage still hands its
    input back unchanged, and the normalize golden fixture is unchanged.
  - No contract byte changes, no versions record is edited, no registry data or
    registry gate is edited, and no gate is added or weakened.

`docs/adr/candidate-choice.md` is accepted and it gives this its own section,
"Which facts can answer the period asked for", ahead of the four steps that use
it. The separation is the record's, not this task's: step 1 asks whether every
fact a rule needs answers the period, and everything above that step is a
question about facts and dates alone, answerable with no other rule in hand.

The strictness is the part to read twice, because it is the part a run is most
likely to soften. Equality on dates, with no window: the record argues it from
the fixture rather than from taste, since one filer carries durations of 24, 90,
91, 92, 182, 183, 273, 274, 365 and 366 days, so "any rule looser than equality
has a year-to-date figure or a prior-year comparative sitting next to the right
answer, under the same tag, in the same filing." A tolerance would be a number
with no source, which anchor 5 bans outright. The same goes for a missing
operand: reading it as zero is reading silence as zero for that component, a
reading the vocabulary already refused where it matters.

The one entry that answers a period other than its own is `answers`, which M4-13
lands and this task is the first to read. It exists for the cover-page share
count — stamped with the filing's own date, in the fixture once per filing and
never at a period end — and the vocabulary priced that in when it wrote "at or
near the period end". What makes it one fact rather than a window is the fetch
surface's own five-field identity, so no search and no nearest-match is needed
here, and none should appear.

Not in this diff: choosing. Two facts under one entry answering one period, and
two rules both answering, are outcomes this task reports and does not settle —
the record settles the first as `Unknown` and the second by its four steps, both
in the task after this one. Reporting them faithfully is what lets that task be
about one thing.
