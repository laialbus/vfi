GOALS.md's fourth M5 criterion ends with "discounted cash flow with margin
of safety". `docs/adr/discounted-cash-flow-and-margin-of-safety.md`,
accepted on 2026-10-19, fixes the whole derivation and names free cash flow
as its base, formed once in a module of its own that three metrics read:
the value per share, `dividend_payout_on_free_cash_flow`, and the
price/free-cash-flow multiple when it is built. Today the payout writes the
subtraction inline. The record says the task that builds the module moves
the payout onto it with identical outcomes, before a second reader exists.

Why a task of its own. The move is a refactor that must change no figure,
and a reviewer checks that best when nothing else moves in the diff. The
settings and the two metrics follow in their own tasks, each on
`crates/analyze/`, so the four are ordered by `depends_on` and never run
together.

It depends on nothing because the record is on main and the module reads
nothing another queued task builds. It runs beside M5-42 and M5-48, which
own one new record file each.

If the payout cannot be moved without a figure, a reason or its entry
changing, or if the Commission's copy on reading forms free cash flow
otherwise than the record says, that is an escalation.
