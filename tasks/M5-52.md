GOALS.md's fourth M5 criterion names "financial health and bankruptcy
scoring, the established screens". `docs/adr/piotroski-signals-and-score.md`,
accepted on 2026-10-20, fixes these two signals: a change in leverage read
at the two years' closes over each year's average assets, with the choice
most worth the decider's judgement, that an unchanged ratio gives zero, made
and argued there; and a change in the current ratio at the two closes. Its
proof section works every figure above by hand, and this task brings the two
signals' share of it.

Why these two together, after M5-51. Both read closing balances of Y and of
Y−1, where Y−1's close is Y's opening, and F_ΔLEVER is the one signal that
averages and the one that reads a balance at three instants; they are the
first metrics to reach `Year::closing_of_before`, and the one read of
`total_assets` at Y's opening serving two averages is the composition the
record singles out. That is a pattern of its own, so it has a run of its own.

It depends on M5-51 because it adds to the module that task extends, and
because both own `crates/analyze/`.

If a signal cannot be built without a seventh reason, a change to a
contract, to `method.rs`, to the entry shape M5-18 fixed, to `settings/`,
to what M5-36 proved of `year.rs`, or to anything M5-45 to M5-51 defined,
if the one read of `total_assets` at Y's opening cannot serve both averages
without a second read or a second reason, or if the copy on reading forms a
signal otherwise than the record, that is an escalation.
