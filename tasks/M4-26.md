`docs/adr/period-alignment.md` is accepted, and one sentence of it was waiting
on the boundary: a filing answers a period through the cover-page entry "only
when that filing's own period of report ends on the period asked for". At v1
the condition could not be stated, so M4-19 refused the entry in every filing
and said why in `filings`: the field it needs is the one the boundary
withholds. M4-25 publishes that field. This is the diff that reads it.

The cost of admitting the entry on nothing was measured on the fixture and is
the reason the condition exists: `shares_outstanding` at 2023-12-31 handed a
count from a cover page nineteen months later, 60,500,000 against the filer's
own 60,000,000 either side of it. The acceptance keeps that instant as the
check that the condition bites — no filing's period of report ends on
2023-12-31, so nothing is admitted and nothing is handed over — and takes
2024-03-31 as the check that it admits: the 10-Q filed 2024-05-08 reports the
quarter ending that day, and with the period-end entry excluded its cover count
is the one candidate. On this filer the exact entry is tagged at every report
date, so with the committed registry nothing the stage answers today changes,
and the case has to be made with a planted registry copy the way M4-18 made
its contests.

One seam M4-17 flagged closes here by where the admission is decided rather
than by a new rule. Under `answers = "filing_reported_in"` the answering module
returns the cover fact whatever period is asked, a duration included; the
strict reading it took otherwise answers a balance only at an instant. The
condition compares a date with the period asked for, so the period asked for
is an instant, and the candidate-choice record already answers a balance only
by one — a duration ending on the report date admits nothing, and a balance is
never keyed under a duration. Where the run finds the record admits another
reading, it stops rather than choosing.

Not in this diff, and the reason it stops here: which of several answering
filings sets the value is Rule 3, and the record states it two ways; the
superseding record is M4-23, parked on a human. Rule 1 asks Rule 3's answer
and waits with it. Nothing here orders filings, parses `filed`, or reads a
form.
