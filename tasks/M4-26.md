Both facts cases in the tree are recordings of the minority. EDGAR spells the
`cik` of a company facts document as a bare number for about nineteen filers in
twenty, and until M4-25 the stage refused every one of those documents, so both
fixtures were chosen from the ten in 217 that spell it as a string — the first
by chance, the second by choice, as its recorder said. Under v2 the majority case
retrieves, and nothing in the tree pins it.

`docs/adr/fetch-normalize-v2.md` says what this case is for, and why it is a
recording rather than a test: no gate compares a field's meaning to what fetch
does with it, so "a later change that padded, truncated or re-spelled the key
while the bytes stood still would be caught by that fixture and by nothing
else". M4-25 pins the read with a document written by hand, which pins the
reader's handling of an integer and not that EDGAR's own document reads. This is
the recording that closes that gap, and it is queued behind M4-25 rather than
inside it because a recording is one run's worth of work on its own and needs
the live source, while publishing a contract needs the label; the two kinds of
risk do not belong in one diff.

Which filer is the run's to choose, and the accepted record names one that
already has a history in the tree — CIK 0001739104, whose filings are
`a-history-longer-than-its-first-page` and whose facts did not retrieve. It is
not required: a long history is a large document, and what the case needs is a
bare-number key on a filer small enough to commit whole. The harness header
already says how a third facts case earns its place; this one earns it on the
spelling, and says so there.

The recording is live and goes out through the recorder under
`crates/fetch/src/bin`, for the reason the harness gives, and sec.gov is not on
the shell's allowlist, so those commands run with the sandbox off as M4-07's and
M4-20's did. Nothing else in the run needs it.

Not in this diff: anything that reads the case. No ruleset turns a third filer's
facts into periods yet, so there is no normalize fixture to write against it.
