GOALS.md's fifth M5 criterion names the dividend streak.
`docs/adr/which-duration-precedes-a-duration.md`, accepted on 2026-10-15,
defines it in full: a count of consecutive paying years ending at the
computing year, at most N, read through the series, stopping at a year not
above zero, at the N-th year or at the first reason, with a zero by silence
read as a year that did not pay. N is the setting `dividend_streak_years`,
preset 10, with the reason the record states; no source fixes the window,
so it is a setting and not a constant, and the record places it under
`settings/` with its metric. The record's own proof lists the streak's
figures on the suspension fixture's years, and this task brings them.

Why the streak first among the year-stated metrics. It reads one concept
and no balance, so it needs no seek beyond `preceding_year`; it carries the
first setting the crate has ever had, which exercises the settings
declaration, the premises and the refusing constructor for the first time;
and its formula is fixed by the record to the last step, where the growth
rate's formula and its tolerance of a one-period excess are left to that
metric's task and have no open source yet.

It depends on M5-36 because it reads the year test and the series that task
builds, and because both own `crates/analyze/`.

If the metric cannot be built without a seventh reason, a change to a
contract, to `method.rs`, to the entry shape M5-18 fixed, or to anything
M5-36 defined, or if the first setting cannot be carried through the
premises as `what-analyze-takes-and-returns.md` states, that is an
escalation.
