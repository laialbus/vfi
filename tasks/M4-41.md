The 2026-09-18 decider sweep flagged that `stands` builds each answering
filing's silence zero as a `Value` and hands every one of them to `latest`, so
two same-day filings that are both silent tie and come to `Unknown`. The owner's
record rules that the silence reading is made once over the period and never
enters the contest, which is what the accepted silence record already says the
vocabulary hands Rule 3.

Ordered after M4-36 because both own this crate, and M4-38 now depends on this
task so that what it crosses into v2 is the ruled shape.

Not in this diff: anything about which periods exist, which filings answer, or
what a read value's tie carries, all of which stand as accepted.
