GOALS.md's fourth M5 criterion names "financial health and bankruptcy
scoring, the established screens". `docs/adr/piotroski-signals-and-score.md`,
accepted on 2026-10-20, fixes these four signals: three changes in a flow
ratio between a year and the year before, each reading the opening balance
where it scales by assets, and the equity-issue signal read through the
weighted diluted count with its blind spots named. Its proof section works
every figure above by hand, and this task brings the four signals' share of
it.

Why these four together, after M5-50. Each reads a flow at Y and at Y−1,
and where it reads a balance it reads the opening instant of each year, so
they are the first metrics to reach the year before through `Year::before`
and `Year::opening_of_before`, and they share that one new pattern; F_ΔROA
reuses the ROA M5-50 formed, which is why it follows that task. F_ΔLEVER
and F_ΔLIQUID read closing balances and averages and have the next task.

It depends on M5-50 because it adds to the module that task creates and
calls the function it formed, and because both own `crates/analyze/`.

If a signal cannot be built without a seventh reason, a change to a
contract, to `method.rs`, to the entry shape M5-18 fixed, to `settings/`,
to what M5-36 proved of `year.rs`, or to anything M5-45 to M5-50 defined,
if the year before's reads cannot be composed in the record's order without
changing another metric's reads, or if the copy on reading forms a signal
otherwise than the record, that is an escalation.
