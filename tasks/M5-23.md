GOALS.md's fifth M5 criterion names payout on earnings and coverage.
`docs/adr/canonical-concepts.md` names these two as the consumers their
concepts were admitted for: `earnings_per_share_diluted` is "consumed by
... dividend coverage and payout in their per-share form", and
`dividends_declared_per_share` by "payout and coverage in their per-share
form". `docs/adr/canonical-concepts-open-questions.md` fixes what each
carries: the dividend is "regular and special dividends together", the
earnings are from continuing operations and, being diluted earnings per
share as the filer reports them, already after preferred dividends, so "the
per-share forms ... are already common-specific" and read no
`preferred_dividends`. Both are flows at a duration period and apply to
every kind, so each metric reads one row and nothing across periods, and
neither has a constant, a setting or a price.

No price is put beside either figure, so the share-basis rule the accepted
analyze record states for a price does not apply: the two per-share figures
cross on one row of the history, each set for that period by the rule
`period-alignment.md` fixes. Dividing one by the other needs no share count
and reads none.

The dividends pair reads zero only where the period is silent on both
members, so a `dividends_declared_per_share` of zero is a period in which
nothing was declared: a payout of zero is a figure, and a coverage of
nothing is undefined and `declined`. A loss year gives a negative payout
and a negative coverage, each the exact figure of its formula, for the
reason M5-22 states.

It depends on M5-22 because each metric adds an entry of the shape M5-18
fixed, and because every metric task so far owns `crates/analyze/`, so
they are ordered rather than run together.

If the hand-over cannot carry these without a change to a contract, to
`method.rs`, to `settings/`, to what M5-17 defines or to the entry shape
M5-18 fixed, that is an escalation.
