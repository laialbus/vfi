GOALS.md's fifth M5 criterion opens its list of dividend metrics with "yield
and relative yield", and its seventh says "Every free parameter arrives as a
setting. Every default states its reason."
`docs/adr/relative-dividend-yield.md`, accepted on 2026-11-23 and Accepted on
main since #436, names the one free parameter of relative yield, the count of
past years whose yields are averaged, and fixes its meaning, unit, domain and
preset, with the preset's reason as the record's own decision.
`docs/adr/what-analyze-takes-and-returns.md` (Accepted) places every setting
in `crates/analyze/src/settings/` behind a constructor that refuses and never
clamps, and M5-59 built the dividend discount valuation's three there, the
last settings added.

Why a task of its own. Adding a field to `Settings` rewrites the pinned
settings list in every test file that carries it, twenty-eight files under
`crates/analyze/tests/` on main and more once M5-70 and M5-71 add theirs,
which is a diff a reviewer wants alone. M5-59 was cut from its metrics for
the same reason, its task recording that the decider asked for that split on
#381. The derivation that reads the setting is M5-74's.

It depends on M5-71 because both own `crates/analyze/`, and because M5-70 and
M5-71 each pin a hand-over's premises at "the nine settings M5-59 left": the
tenth lands after them, so neither acceptance is made false while its task is
in flight. It reads nothing either task builds.

If the setting cannot be carried through the premises as the analyze record
states, if the domain cannot be refused without a literal outside
`settings/`, if the count cannot be held within `u16` as the record requires,
or if the page on reading no longer carries the sentence quoted, that is an
escalation.
