GOALS.md's fourth M5 criterion names financial health and bankruptcy
scoring and the established screens, and its fifth the dividend growth rate
and streak. Every one of them reads a year, the year before it, or a series
of years, and `docs/adr/which-duration-precedes-a-duration.md`, accepted on
2026-10-15, is the rule they read under: a year from its own dates under 26
U.S.C. §441, alone among year-length spans, the year before by equality of
the opening date with an end, the series as that seek repeated, two more
`sought_by` names and three more conditions. The record names "the task
that implements the rule, alone or with the first metric that reads it".
This task is that one, alone, as M5-28 built the date reading before any
metric read it.

Why alone. The rule is the larger half of any metric that reads it: two
length clauses, the alone clause, the seek, the series, three operations in
`date.rs`, a constants file and the two fixtures' full tables. The first
metric, the streak on M5-37, adds the first setting the crate has ever
carried, which is its own run. Splitting here keeps each diff reviewable,
and the proof is split by what each can show: this task proves the rule on
its own answers, and M5-37 proves the first metric on it.

It depends on M5-35 because the module sits beside M5-29's seeks and reads
through M5-28's `date.rs`, and because every task in the chain owns
`crates/analyze/`.

If the rule cannot be built without a seventh reason, a fourth number, a
date read or ordered outside `date.rs`, a change to a contract, to
`method.rs`, to `settings/` or to the entry shape M5-18 fixed, that is an
escalation.
