The vocabulary is published at v1: twenty-eight concepts, five filer kinds,
three states. The boundary that carries facts into normalize is published too.
Between them sits the thing GOALS.md asks for by name — "mapping happens through
a versioned registry with per-company overrides — data, not branching code" —
and nothing decides its shape. Until it does, the mapping cannot be built
without inventing that shape mid-run, which is how the branching pile the anchor
bans gets written one plausible `if` at a time.

It is a proposal rather than an implementation because the registry is a
versioned data surface between the vocabulary and the stage that reads it, and
because a per-company override is a standing exception to a published contract.
Both are above a worker. The home is the sharpest part of the decision: the
layout doc is the single source of truth for placement, and a registry that
lands somewhere invented mid-run is a file the next agent will not find. If the
record concludes the registry belongs under a protected path, that is what it
says — publishing it there is a later task with a signature, not a reason to put
it somewhere else.

Two things this record must not settle, both because they are separately named
in GOALS.md and both because settling them here would bury a methodology
judgement inside a data-format decision. Which of several candidates in one
filing wins — consolidated over segment, and the right period basis — is one.
How amendments, restatements and period alignment reconcile is the other, and
GOALS.md says in the criterion itself that it is separate from the mapping. Name
them; leave them.

Independent of the two tasks queued beside it: this touches no crate, no
manifest and no published surface, and nothing in it is shaped by what the first
company-facts fixture turns out to hold.
