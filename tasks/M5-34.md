GOALS.md's fifth M5 criterion names retention, and until 2026-10-14 no
record stated it as a formula: the catalogue names it a consumer of
`net_income` and `preferred_dividends` and of nothing carrying a dividend,
so a total form would have added a consumer no record names.
`docs/adr/common-shareholder-forms.md`, accepted that day, settles it per
share: one less the per-share payout M5-23 already computes, over the one
common pair v2 carries, with the two CFA texts as its source and
`zero_earnings_per_share_diluted` shared with M5-23. The record says of it
that the task "has nothing left to decide but its code".

It reads no balance, so it waits on no seek and no instant. It depends on
M5-33 only because both own `crates/analyze/`, and it sits before M5-35,
which multiplies it by return on common equity.

If the metric cannot be built without a constant, a seventh reason, a
change to a contract, to `method.rs`, to `settings/` or to the entry shape
M5-18 fixed, that is an escalation.
