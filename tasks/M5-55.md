GOALS.md's fourth M5 criterion ends with "discounted cash flow with margin
of safety", and its ninth says a metric with no price data is "absent with
a reason" and "never shows a wrong number".
`docs/adr/discounted-cash-flow-and-margin-of-safety.md`, accepted on
2026-10-19, fixes the margin's form, its date, its two conditions, how it
composes on the value and the price, and the proof above; M5-47 built the
value it is built on. `docs/adr/share-basis-rule-dates.md`, accepted on
2026-10-26, fixes the comparisons the rule makes and the condition a date
outside the grammar gives, and names the margin as the one metric that
lists it today. Its proof section is written through the margin, and this
task brings it through the hand-over.

Why the margin now. The last two plans held it for the share-basis record,
which is on main. It is the first metric to read a price, so it is the
first the ninth criterion can be checked against: a user with no key sees
the value per share and the reason the margin is missing, and no number.

It depends on M5-54 because it calls the two functions that task builds and
drops the expectations that task leaves, and because both own
`crates/analyze/`.

If the margin cannot be built without a seventh reason, a change to a
contract, to `method.rs`, to the entry shape M5-18 fixed, to `settings/`,
to what M5-54 built, or to anything M5-45 to M5-53 defined, if the crossing
cannot reach the derivation without another metric's reads changing, or if
a case in either record's table cannot be given the outcome the record
states, that is an escalation.
