`docs/adr/period-alignment.md` is accepted, and its Rule 3 does not agree with
itself. Its Decision says: "among the answering filings whose attempt produced a
`Value`: the value is the one from the filing with the greatest `filed`, and the
`Value` names that filing" — every value, whether or not the filings differ. Its
Enforcement section, writing the restatement fixture that same rule must pass,
says the nine mapped concepts that agree across the two filings must resolve
"naming *that* accession" — the earlier one — "because those filings agree and
Rule 3 has nothing to move", and that "a fixture that showed all ten switching
to the later filing would be recording a rule nobody stated". Its tag-change
paragraph takes the first reading again: "Rule 3 takes the later filing, so the
resolved value records the later tag even when the number did not change."

Both cannot hold, and the merged fixture is where they part. At the half-year
2023-10-01 to 2024-03-31 the 10-Q filed 2024-05-08 and the 10-Q filed 2025-05-14
carry every mapped element the other does, identical except diluted EPS; under
the Decision every one of them names accession `0001213900-25-042964`, which is
the outcome the Enforcement section rejects. Which reading holds decides the
recorded provenance of every agreeing value in the corpus — and, for the filer
that changed tags, whether provenance moves at all — so no run should pick one.
The 2026-09-06 plan flagged it, and the decider has carried it in three sweeps
with one recommendation: a superseding record for Rule 3 alone, Structural, so
that it can be accepted without a human, ahead of whatever implements it.

That is what this is. Accepted records are never edited, so the fix is a new
record that states Rule 3 whole — the choice and its three clauses — and leaves
the other two rules, the fiscal-year decision and the v2 proposal standing where
they are. The reading it takes is the proposer's to argue and the decider's to
weigh; the acceptance above asks only that it be one reading, stated once,
checked against the fixture, and that whatever it prefers by is a date, a form
or an accession and never a number.

Nothing implements it here. Rule 3 in code, and Rule 1 behind it — which asks
"at least one concept its kind admits resolves to a `Value`", the settled
answer — wait on this record being accepted, and so does the restatement fixture
the milestone names. This depends on nothing in the queue beside it: it reads
accepted records and merged fixtures, all on main, and touches no crate, no
manifest, no gate and no data.
