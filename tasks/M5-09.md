Anchor 5's enforcement clause, and GOALS.md's sixth and seventh M5 criteria,
that every methodology constant is named, defined once and cited, and that
every free parameter arrives as a setting. ANCHORS.md says what holds them is
"a lint on the analyze crate" that "bans bare numeric literals outside a small
allowlist", running in CI. `docs/adr/what-analyze-takes-and-returns.md` is
accepted, and under "Where a literal may stand" it gives the lint its three
places and leaves the allowlist and whether tests are read to "the lint's own
task". Under Enforcement it names the lint as not built and names the preset
constructor as a gap the lint's task can close. This is the record for that
lint.

`docs/adr/TEMPLATE.md` puts a new gate in the Structural tier, so the record
comes before the gate, as M5-08 does for the key check and as the decider's
flag on M5-04 says of a gate with no record of its own. The lint comes before
the first metric, because AGENTS.md makes a change with no gate an escalation
and a metric is a derivation that carries constants. The task that builds the
check is queued under this record's acceptance; it will own `scripts/gates.sh`
and so sit after M5-04, which owns the same file. This record does not wait on
M5-04, because it reads for a different thing and shares no list with it; it
only has to say how the two sit beside each other.

If the work finds the three places cannot hold what the derivation needs
without changing something the accepted record decided, that is an
escalation.
