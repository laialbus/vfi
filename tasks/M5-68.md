GOALS.md's fifth M5 criterion opens its list of dividend metrics with "yield
and relative yield", and its ninth says a metric with no price data is
"absent with a reason" and "never shows a wrong number".
`docs/adr/dividend-yield.md`, accepted on 2026-11-20 and Accepted on main
since #427, fixes the yield to the last step: a year's dividends declared
per common share over the unadjusted close at the valuation date, one
division, two conditions reached from where they stand, the price read
through M5-54's one function with E the end of Y, and a proof worked by
hand. This task has only its code left to decide.

It is the shape `price_to_earnings_per_share` has, one figure read beside
the price read, with the close as the divisor, so the dividend's condition
is asked before the close's, as both margins ask their figure's first.

The arm. The decider's entry of 2026-11-20 notes that no task has licensed
the lift of the arm composing a read's reasons with the price read's. It
stands in five files on main, and the yield would hold a seventh copy after
M5-65's sixth. This task licenses the lift and does not require
it: the worker judges whether one function serves every reader without
changing an order of reasons, and where it does not, the yield holds the
arm as the others do.

It depends on M5-65 because both own `crates/analyze/`, and because the
name lists it appends to are the ones M5-65 leaves.

Relative yield is not this task's. It has no record, and the past period
ends are read by no metric here.

If the metric cannot be built without a seventh reason, a change to a
contract, to `method.rs`, to the entry shape M5-18 fixed, to `settings/`, to
what M5-54 built beyond the lift licensed above, or to anything M5-45 to
M5-65 defined beyond that lift, if a case in the record's proof cannot be
given the outcome the record states, or if a source on reading says
otherwise than the record, that is an escalation.
