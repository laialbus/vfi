GOALS.md's fifth M5 criterion ends with "dividend-based valuation", and its
ninth says a metric with no price data is "absent with a reason" and "never
shows a wrong number".
`docs/adr/dividend-discount-valuation-and-margin-of-safety.md`, accepted on
2026-11-06, fixes the margin's form by adopting the discounted cash flow
record's, its date, its one condition, how it composes on the value and the
price read, and the proof above, including the one exposure it leaves open
and asks to be pinned. `docs/adr/share-basis-rule-dates.md` (Accepted) fixes
the comparisons the rule makes and the condition a date outside the grammar
gives.

Why the margin now. It is the second metric to read a price, and the first
to be built on a form another metric already wrote, so its proof is the
record's margin table read through M5-54's reading and M5-55's form, and
nothing of its own but the composition. A user with no key sees the value
and the reason the margin is missing.

It depends on M5-60 because it calls the one function that task builds, and
on M5-54 and M5-55 through it, because it reaches the reading and the form
those tasks wrote, and because all of them own `crates/analyze/`.

If the margin cannot be built without a seventh reason, a change to a
contract, to `method.rs`, to the entry shape M5-18 fixed, to `settings/`, to
what M5-54 or M5-60 built, or to anything M5-45 to M5-60 defined beyond
lifting the form out of M5-55's derivation unchanged, if the crossing cannot
reach the derivation without another metric's reads changing, if the form
cannot be shared without a pinned figure of the discounted cash flow margin
moving, or if a case in either record's table cannot be given the outcome
the record states, that is an escalation.
