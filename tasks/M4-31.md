M4-27 landed the fourth filer whose facts are in the tree, CIK 0002011954,
recorded for a filer whose liabilities exceed its assets — one of the golden
fixtures the milestone names. Two of the four have a kind; this one does not.
This is the task M4-21 and M4-22 were for the first two, queued on its own
rather than left to the fixture that will be written against it, for the
reason M4-22 gave: a kind is read off the filer's balance sheet and income
statement against the vocabulary's clause, and a fixture's expected result is
read off the ruleset, and the two are decided against different evidence.

The fixture needs the kind settled first. Rule 1 of the accepted
`docs/adr/period-alignment.md` makes a filer's period set a function of it — "a
period is the filer's when at least one concept its kind admits resolves to a
`Value` at it" — so an expected result written for a filer with no kind would be
written against a set that changes the day one is asserted. A filer with a kind
is also a filer that can reach `NotApplicable`, one of the three states the
milestone requires and one no test may reach by naming a kind for itself.

What is particular to this filer is that its equity is stated under two
elements across its history, and that the fixture exists for a sign. Neither
is evidence of a shape. The vocabulary says a negative amount is a value like
any other, and a change in the element a filer states its equity under is not
in the clause for any kind. But the vocabulary also says a kind holds for every
period, so the argument reads both sides and refuses to assert over a pivot,
exactly as M4-22 read both calendars. An unassigned filer costs precision and
never produces a wrong answer; a kind asserted where the facts disagree
produces the wrong answer this milestone exists to prevent.

Not in this diff: anything that reads the case through the stage, which waits
on the stage being wired; and any kind for the filers M4-28 and M4-29 record,
which are queued once those recordings are merged.
