GOALS.md's fourth M5 criterion names "valuation multiples" among the value
metrics. The catalogue names EV/EBIT and EV/EBITDA as consumers of
`operating_income` and `depreciation_and_amortization`, and enterprise value
as a consumer of market capitalisation and of net debt; the provider record
lists all three under the valuation date; and three accepted records, the
capital record, the common-shareholder record and the market capitalisation
record, each leave enterprise value to a record of its own by name. None
forms it.

Why a record, and not the metrics' tasks. Whether enterprise value counts the
preferred claim makes `preferred_equity` a consumer the open-questions record
did not admit, which is a decision above a worker by the common-shareholder
record's own words. Enterprise value is one term two multiples read, built
on two terms other metrics already read, so where it is formed and in what
order its reads are made is decided once. The instant its balances are read
at meets the period record's averaging sentence a third time, after the
bridge and the averaged count. EBITDA has no reader and no home on main. Each
is a reading a worker must not guess, and one record settles them with their
reasons, as the market capitalisation record settled its own.

Why now. The market capitalisation record was accepted on 2026-11-08, and
its term is what earlier plans held this record for. Chapter 18 and the 2006
survey opened whole in this run at the digests that record names, and the
pages on the firm value multiple were read.

It depends on nothing because every record it reads is accepted and on main.
It reads the market capitalisation module by the place and form the accepted
record gives it, not by M5-63's code. It owns one new file, so it runs beside
M5-63 to M5-65 on `crates/analyze/`. The multiples are queued under the
record's acceptance, not here.

If the record finds a form cannot be stated without an operation outside the
arithmetic record's table, a new reason, a change to a frozen contract's
bytes, a v3 concept or a `unit` the entry shape does not carry, it says so by
name as the acceptance above provides. If it cannot be written at all without
an accepted record changing, that is an escalation.
