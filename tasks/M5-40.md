GOALS.md's fifth M5 criterion names the dividend growth rate.
`docs/adr/which-duration-precedes-a-duration.md`, accepted on 2026-10-15,
fixes everything about it but the formula: it reads n + 1 years through the
series, at least the first and the last; n is the setting
`dividend_growth_years`, preset 5, with the reason the record states; a
zero at the base year and a negative figure are its own `declined`
conditions; and how it tolerates the one-period excess is "that metric's
task". The arithmetic record already carries the n-th root "which a
compound growth rate over n years needs", with the index from a setting
and a negative radicand and a zero index as the metric's own conditions.

Why the geometric average, and why now. Two planning runs held this metric
because no open source for its form had been found. This run found one the
sources record admits: the author's own chapter, published on his own site,
which sets the geometric average as the first and last observations'
quotient raised to one over n less one, says in words that it reads only
those two and ignores the intermediate observations, and says it is not
meaningful at a zero or negative base. That is the record's "reads at
least the first and the last" and its two `declined` conditions, stated by
a source. Reading only the two ends is also what tolerates the excess the
catalogue hands M5: an excess at a year between moves nothing, and one at
either end moves the rate through one step of n, which is the reason the
record gives the preset.

Why after M5-39. It reads the year test and the series M5-36 builds, adds
the second field beside the setting M5-37 adds, and every task in the chain
owns `crates/analyze/`; M5-39 is the last queued before it.

If the metric cannot be built without a seventh reason, a new arithmetic
operation, a change to a contract, to `method.rs`, to the entry shape M5-18
fixed, or to anything M5-36 or M5-37 defined, or if a source on reading
says otherwise than this task states it, that is an escalation.
