The emit record priced the wrong-shape zeros — 45 of the restatement filer's 96
— and said the shape that avoids them was not its to choose, because two
accepted records pinned such rows; the owner's record supersedes those rows and
judges the zero unsupported. This task makes the reading follow.

Ordered after M4-41 because both touch how the period's zero is supplied in
`stands`, and M4-38 now depends on this task so the counts it pins are the
ruled ones.

Not in this diff: the emit shape, which still carries every concept at every
period; anything about which periods exist; and the vocabulary's wording, which
the record leaves to its next version.
