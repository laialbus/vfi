`docs/adr/dividend-discount-valuation-and-margin-of-safety.md`, accepted on
2026-11-06, fixes the derivation to the last step: the stable-growth form
over the mean of n years' declared dividends, four operations from the
arithmetic record's table, a growth rate read from a setting and never from
the computed rate, three `declined` conditions and their order, a zero at Y
as a condition, and how absence composes over n reads. Its proof section
works every figure above by hand, and this task brings them; the planner
reproduced each in this run in exact decimal arithmetic.

Why the value before the margin. The record says "The value per share reads
no price and is computed where the margin is absent." It needs no crossing
and no share-basis rule, so a user with no key sees it. It does need the
three settings, which M5-59 defines, and the series reading M5-36 and M5-49
built.

It depends on M5-59 because it reads the settings that task defines, and
because both own `crates/analyze/`.

The split inside the window that the record names under "What no check can
see" is the record's open item and not this task's to close: the value reads
no crossing and cannot see it.

beyond what M5-59 left, to the arithmetic record's table or to anything
