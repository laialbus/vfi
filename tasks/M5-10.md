    benchmark baseline is not re-recorded.
  - '`vfi-contracts` still depends on nothing, and no entry enters or leaves

  - No file outside the two crates changes.

`docs/adr/what-analyze-takes-and-returns.md`, accepted on 2026-10-03, decides
the type analyze compiles against. Normalize's `History` and `Row` move to
`vfi-contracts::canonical_concepts` unchanged in shape, normalize builds them,
analyze compiles against them, the module carries the comparison test against
v2's `carries` line, and v2's bytes stay as they are. This is that move, and
nothing else the record decides.

It runs alone because the crate beside the contract files is a shared
surface, as every task that owned it has run (M4-15, M4-37). It needs no label,
because nothing under `contracts/` changes.

If the move cannot be made without changing a v2 byte or the type's shape, that
is an escalation.
