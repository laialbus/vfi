GOALS.md's fourth, fifth, sixth, seventh and ninth M5 criteria each need a
figure computed and written. `docs/adr/what-analyze-takes-and-returns.md` is
accepted, and under "What this does not decide" it says "the arithmetic
analyze computes in, and how a figure is written" go "to the first metric
task, under the method version". `analyze-store` v1's `[elsewhere]` says the
same of `how_a_figure_is_written`. A `Value` outcome is the figure "as the
characters analyze writes", and a history amount reaches analyze as the
characters the document published, so between the two stands a reading, an
arithmetic and a writing that no record has decided. This is the record for
them.

Why a record, and not the first metric's code. `docs/adr/bare-literal-lint.md`
names the written precision as a literal that fits none of its three places
and says "the task that first needs one escalates". Its Consequences make a
fourth place Constitutional, and the decider's review of it calls a cited
constant that cites nothing the exact defect anchor 5 names. So a metric task
coded first stops exactly where those records say it stops. One record, before
any metric, means forty metric tasks share one arithmetic and one written form
and none of them chooses. This is the shape M5-06 and M5-09 took before their
implementing tasks.

It depends on nothing because everything it reads is on main, which is the
two accepted records and the three frozen contracts. `Resolution::Value`
carries its amount and `Outcome::Value` its figure as characters already, in
`vfi-contracts`, under M5-10 and M5-12. The analyze skeleton M5-15 builds is a
premise by that task's acceptance, which this record leaves standing, and the
record touches nothing M5-14 or M5-15 owns.

The first metric task waits on this record's acceptance, and through it every
metric and the share-basis rule. Those are queued under this record's
acceptance, not here.

If the work finds the figure cannot be written without a seventh reason, a
change to a frozen contract's bytes, or a widening of the lint, the record
says so by name as the acceptance above provides. If it cannot be written at
all without an accepted record changing, that is an escalation.
