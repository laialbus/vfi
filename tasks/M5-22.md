GOALS.md's fifth M5 criterion names payout on earnings and on free cash
flow, and coverage. `docs/adr/canonical-concepts.md` names these as the
consumers their concepts were admitted for: `dividends_paid` is "consumed
by dividend payout on free cash flow and on earnings in their total form;
dividend coverage", `net_income` by "dividend payout on earnings" among
others, `operating_cash_flow` by "free cash flow, and through it ...
dividend payout on free cash flow", and `capital_expenditure` by "free cash
flow". `docs/adr/canonical-concepts-open-questions.md` fixes the form:
`dividends_paid` is cash paid to all classes of shareholder, so "payout on
free cash flow and coverage in their total form are then all-holder
measures", internally consistent over `net_income` as v2 carries it, and
"M5 has both" forms. The common-shareholder forms that read
`preferred_dividends` are not these, and are not queued here. All four
concepts are flows at a duration period and apply to every kind, so each
metric reads one row and nothing across periods, and none has a constant,
a setting or a price.

`capital_expenditure` crosses as a magnitude, positive when cash was spent,
so free cash flow is the difference and not a sum. Its silence reads
`Unknown` by the parent record's own reasoning, that a zero "would overstate
free cash flow by the whole of it", so a filer whose capital expenditure the
registry did not resolve carries the free-cash-flow payout as absent with
`input_unknown`, which is the correct absence and not a failure.

The dividends pair reads zero only where the period is silent on both
members, so a `dividends_paid` of zero is a period in which nothing was
paid: a payout of zero is a figure, and a coverage of nothing is undefined
and `declined`. A loss year gives a negative payout, which is the exact
figure of the formula; a sign condition would be the first comparison in
the derivation, and the one such condition the accepted analyze record
names, price/earnings on non-positive earnings, belongs to that metric's
task.

It depends on M5-21 because each metric adds an entry of the shape M5-18
fixed, and because every metric task so far owns `crates/analyze/`, so
they are ordered rather than run together.

If the hand-over cannot carry these without a change to a contract, to
`method.rs`, to `settings/`, to what M5-17 defines or to the entry shape
M5-18 fixed, that is an escalation.
