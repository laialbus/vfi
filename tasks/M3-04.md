M3 asks for a funnel — seed set, then metadata gate, then history for survivors
— and for every filer it evaluates to have a verdict and a reason on record.
Neither can be built until the ledger has a shape, and the ledger's shape is not
a worker's to pick: ANCHORS names it among the components many stages depend on,
which are reached only through an explicit interface, and if its records persist
then a storage schema comes with it. Both are above a worker, so the proposal
comes first and the funnel follows the decision.

The hard part is the one anchor 2 creates: the stage that produces the verdicts
is the earliest stage in the pipeline, and it may not call the one that stores
things. Say how that is resolved, not that it is.
