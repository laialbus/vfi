---
id: M1-07
title: Verify a claim after the push, release it on conflict
milestone: M1
owns:
  - scripts/tasks.sh
depends_on: [M1-06]
exclusive: no
acceptance:
  - One supported entry point performs a claim. It pushes the task's branch,
    then re-reads origin. If the claim now coexists with one it must not — an
    exclusive claim alongside any other claim, in either direction — the run
    deletes its own branch and reports the claim as lost.
  - However two racing claims interleave, once both re-checks settle origin is
    never left with an exclusive claim and another claim in flight together,
    and a released claim leaves no branch behind.
  - A claim with no conflict succeeds unchanged, and `available` behaves
    exactly as before.
---

This is the read-time race from the decider's reviews of PR #18: `available`
checks claims at read time, so two runs can both see an idle queue and both
push, and the exclusive rule is violated the moment the second ref lands. The
claim mechanism in WORKPLAN.md — branch push, atomic per ref — is unchanged;
this adds the verification step after it. WORKPLAN.md is protected and nothing
here touches it. Both sides releasing and retrying is acceptable; both
proceeding is not.
