GOALS.md's fourth M5 criterion names returns on capital and valuation
multiples, and its fifth names payout on earnings, coverage, retention and
sustainable growth. `docs/adr/canonical-concepts-open-questions.md` fixes
that "M5's metrics are common-shareholder measures", admits
`preferred_equity` and `preferred_dividends` for exactly the common forms of
these, and states one of the three terms: common equity is
`shareholders_equity` less `preferred_equity`. It states the second only by
definition, since `preferred_dividends` is "the deduction between the
period's profit and the profit available to common shareholders". It states
the third not at all. `dividends_paid` is "all classes of shareholder,
common and preferred", consumed by the total forms M5-22 computed and by
nothing else; `dividends_declared_per_share` is per common share, consumed
by the per-share forms M5-23 computes; and no concept carries the common
dividend as a total. Retention is named a consumer of `net_income` and of
`preferred_dividends` and of nothing that carries a dividend, so its
dividend term is this same gap.

Why a record, and not the first common form's code. Forming the common
dividend from `dividends_paid` less `preferred_dividends` subtracts a figure
declared for the period, with accretion and redemption adjustments, from a
figure paid in cash during it, and whether that difference is the common
dividend or a wrong number that looks like one is a method choice with a
source, not a derivation a metric file can make alone. Return on equity,
payout, coverage, retention and sustainable growth each read one or more of
the three terms, and five metric tasks forming them would be five readings
of one rule, which anchor 5 and the one-source-of-truth invariant forbid.
If the honest answer is that v2 cannot form the common dividend, the fix is
a v3 concept, which is a contract change above a worker, and this record is
where that is found out and named rather than guessed around. The 2026-10-10
and 2026-10-13 plans held every common form and retention for this.

It depends on M5-26 because return on equity and sustainable growth pair a
flow with a balance, and the rule for which period the balance is read at
is that record's. With it on main, this record names the rule and decides
nothing of it, so two records never read a date two ways. The two own
different files, so the ordering is for one rule and not for a collision.

The common forms themselves, retention and sustainable growth are queued
under this record's acceptance, not here.

If the record finds the forms cannot be stated without a seventh reason, a
change to a frozen contract's bytes, or a widening of the lint, it says so
by name as the acceptance above provides. If it cannot be written at all
without an accepted record changing, that is an escalation.
