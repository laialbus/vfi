This is the vocabulary the rest of M4 resolves into and every metric in M5
computes over. It is above a worker twice: it is the normalize → analyze
contract, and ANCHORS.md names the canonical concept definitions among the
surfaces that change only with sign-off.

The criterion doing the work is that the set holds exactly what analysis needs
and nothing else. That makes it a derivation, not a catalogue — read what M5
says will be computed and the concepts are what those metrics consume. A set
assembled from what filings publish would be larger, and every surplus concept
is a mapping rule somebody has to get right for a number nobody reads.

The other hard part is the difference between "not applicable" and "unknown". A
bank has no gross profit; a mapping that failed also produces no gross profit.
Downstream those two look identical unless the set makes them different, and one
of them is a defect. Say how they are kept apart by the shape of the answer, not
by which code path happened to run.

Nothing here is guessed. Where the meaning of a concept is genuinely contested —
where two defensible readings would produce different numbers — record it as
open with what it turns on, rather than picking one and moving on. GOALS.md
calls a wrong answer here the failure mode that looks right, and an ADR is the
cheapest place in the project to be wrong out loud.
