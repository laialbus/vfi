GOALS.md's fourth M5 criterion names financial health and bankruptcy
scoring and the established screens, and its fifth names the dividend
growth rate and streak. Piotroski's five change signals compare a year
with the year before, Altman and Piotroski state their scores over a year,
and the growth rate and streak read a run of years. None of these can be
computed until a metric can find the year before a year, and the accepted
period record says in so many words that its rule does not do that:
"finding the year before a year means telling a year from a quarter, a
year-to-date span and a transition stub", which "needs a fiscal year end
or a length class that no source here fixes", and so it "is left to a
record of its own". The accepted period-alignment record stops at the
same place, "rather than inventing a class", and says the answer may need
a fiscal year end, "a decision and another version bump".

Why a record, and not the first score's code. Whether a duration is a
year, and which duration is the year before it, is exactly the question
both accepted records refused to answer in passing, in the one part of the
project where a plausible wrong answer is the failure mode. Eight metrics
reading it would be eight readings of one rule. If the honest answer is
that the history cannot say without a fact normalize does not carry, the
fix is a contract change above a worker, and this record is where that is
found out and named rather than guessed around. The same record is where
the series the dividend metrics read is decided, so that two records do
not find a preceding duration two ways, and it reads dates through the one
module the accepted record fixes.

It depends on nothing because everything it reads is on main: the two
accepted records that name it, the fixture that shows the hard case, the
frozen contracts and the entry shape. The scores, the signals, the growth
rate and the streak are queued under this record's acceptance, not here.

If the record finds the rule cannot be stated without a seventh reason, a
change to a frozen contract's bytes, or a widening of the lint, it says so
by name as the acceptance above provides. If it cannot be written at all
without an accepted record changing, that is an escalation.
