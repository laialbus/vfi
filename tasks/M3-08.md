acceptance:
  - The funnel runs end to end in one pass: seed set, then metadata gate, then
    history retrieval — for the admitted filers and for no one else.
  - No filer the metadata gate rejected has its history retrieved. This is
    checked by a case that would catch it, not asserted in a comment.
  - The set the history step runs over is derived from the verdicts recorded,
    not carried alongside them.
  - Every filer the history step evaluates has a verdict recorded: its history
    retrieved, nothing filed, or not judged because the retrieval failed. A
    failed retrieval is never a rejection, and "nothing filed" is a correct
    absence rather than a failure.
  - One pass is identified on every verdict it records, so the verdicts of an
    interrupted pass are distinguishable from those of the last complete one
    without inferring it from timestamps.
  - A pass interrupted part way leaves the journal readable and every verdict it
    completed intact, and says nothing about the filers it never reached.
  - The whole funnel is pinned by golden fixtures under `fixtures/fetch/`,
    recorded from live EDGAR and committed as data.
  - Nothing proved gets narrower: M3-07's criteria and cases still hold, every
    existing gate and proof still passes, and every request still leaves through
    M3-01's chokepoint.
  - No test reaches the live network.

This closes M3's funnel criterion. The retrieval itself already exists — M3-03's
`filing_history` is what the third step runs — so the work here is the step
boundary and the verdicts, not a second way to fetch a history.

The criterion that carries the weight is the negative one: that a rejected filer
is never fetched. A funnel that retrieves everything and discards the rejects
afterwards passes every other line above and fails the only one that matters,
because the point of the funnel is the requests it does not send. Write the case
that would catch that before writing the step.

The pass identifier is what makes an interrupted sweep legible. A pass over the
seed set is long enough to be killed part way, and without it on the record,
which verdicts belong to the last complete sweep is reconstructed from clocks,
which is a guess — and this project does not guess.

What a retrieved history becomes to the next stage is the fetch → normalize
contract and is not settled here. `scripts/gates.sh` is protected and not this
task's to touch.
