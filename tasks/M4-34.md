M4-29 landed the sixth filer whose facts are in the tree, CIK 0002033615,
recorded for the filer whose statements do not fit the ordinary shape — one of
the golden fixtures the milestone names. Four of the six have a kind. This one
is the last of the five a named golden fixture will be written over that has
none, and it is the one the absence turns on: `gross_profit` is the concept
whose `applies_to` is `operating` alone, so until a kind stands the recording
pins an absence of elements and nothing about the third state. This is the task
M4-21, M4-22, M4-31 and M4-33 were for the others, kept on its own for the
reason M4-22 gave — a kind is read off the filer's balance sheet and income
statement against the vocabulary's clause, a fixture's expected result is read
off the ruleset, and the two are decided against different evidence.

The fixture needs the kind settled first. Rule 1 of the accepted
`docs/adr/period-alignment.md` makes a filer's period set a function of the
kind — a period is the filer's when at least one concept its kind admits
resolves to a `Value` at it — and six concepts carry a clause narrower than all
five kinds, so which periods this filer has and which of its concepts reach a
correct absence both move the day one is asserted.

What is particular to this filer is in the tree already, and the argument is
the run's to make from it. The recording carries no fact under
`us-gaap:GrossProfit` and none under any of the four cost-of-revenue elements
the committed registry's difference forms for `gross_profit` subtract, under
any of its taxonomies, so none of the eight filings its facts came from states
one. Its two year-end balance sheets close to the dollar and subtotal no
current section. The harness header says which kind the run that recorded it
read off those facts; this task does not take that reading on trust, because a
kind repeated from a neighbouring file is a kind nobody established.
