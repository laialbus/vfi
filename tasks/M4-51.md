The accepted `golden-claims-and-baseline-re-recording.md` decides that the
normalize golden harness checks a fixture's `claims` against `expected` as
well as `expected` byte for byte. It also says the check "is an exclusive task
with its own record since it touches a gate". Until that record exists, the
decider re-derives every claim by hand. The 2026-09-23 planner stopped short of
this record because M4-44 had not yet fixed the claims form. M4-44 has now
merged it, and three fixtures carry it. They do not carry it identically, and
this record decides what the check will read.

The record touches only its own file. M4-46 and M4-48 may land while it is
written. Whatever `claims` files are on main when the implementing task runs,
that task brings them into the decided form, and this record says how.

Not in this diff: the check itself, any edit to a `claims` file, and the
harness. The planner queues each of those once this record is accepted.
