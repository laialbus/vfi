GOALS.md's fourth M5 criterion names "valuation multiples" among the value
metrics, and its ninth says a metric with no price data is "absent with a
reason" and "never shows a wrong number".
`docs/adr/market-capitalisation-and-the-equity-multiples.md`, accepted on
2026-11-08, forms market capitalisation once in a module of its own, as net
debt and free cash flow are formed, and fixes price/book as the chapter's
total form over the common equity `docs/adr/common-shareholder-forms.md`
(Accepted) already forms, with its name, its instant, its four conditions
and their order, and the proof above.

Why the term and price/book together. The term has no reader until a
multiple reads it, and price/book is the one multiple that reads the term at
a single row, so the first reader and the one function are proved in one
diff and the module never stands with a `dead_code` expectation. Price/sales
and price/free cash flow, which read the term at two instants and average
it, are the next two tasks'. Enterprise value and Altman's X₄ will read the
module too, under records of their own.

Why price/book is the first metric at an instant to read a price. Every
price-dependent metric on main is stated over a year, so `Computing` reaches
the close with E the duration's end alone. The record fixes E for price/book
as the instant's date, and this task is where that reach is extended,
through the same one function, with no second place the crossing is read.

It depends on M5-62 because both own `crates/analyze/`, and it reaches
`non_positive_close` as M5-61 and M5-62 leave it.

The two questions the record leaves open, whether the period record's
averaging sentence reaches a count the price multiplies and whether the
share-basis rule sees a count read before E, do not reach price/book, which
reads one count at one instant. Neither is this task's.

If the term cannot be formed once without a second product elsewhere, if the
close cannot be reached for an instant without a second reading of the
crossing or a change to what M5-54 built beyond its reach, if the metric
cannot be built without a seventh reason, a change to a contract, to
`method.rs`, to the entry shape M5-18 fixed, to `Unit`, to `settings/`, or to
anything M5-45 to M5-62 defined beyond widening a constant's visibility, if
a case in the record's proof cannot be given the outcome the record states,
or if a source on reading says otherwise than the record, that is an
escalation.
