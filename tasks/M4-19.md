`docs/adr/period-alignment.md` is accepted, and this is its Rule 2: "A filing
answers a period when it carries at least one fact whose own period is exactly
that period", and resolution "runs once per period, per concept, per answering
filing, over that filing's facts and no other's."

Both halves are argued there from the fixture rather than from taste. One filing
at a time, because a composition assembled across two filings is a number no
filing states — the objection candidate choice already makes to a `sum` that
mixes a quarter with a year to date. Every answering filing rather than the
newest, because the newest is usually the thinnest: the last filing to mention
the quarter 2024-10-01 to 2024-12-31 carries two facts at it, so a rule that read
that filing alone would resolve `net_income` for the quarter and report revenue,
assets, equity and cash flow absent for a quarter the filer published in full.

The cover-page entry is the one admission this diff has to refuse. The record's
section on the field the boundary withholds sets the condition for admitting an
entry that answers the period of the filing it was reported in — that filing's
own period of report must end on the period asked for — and then says what v1
does with it: "At `fetch-normalize` v1 the condition cannot be stated, so it is
never met and such an entry is never a candidate." What admitting it would cost
is measured on this fixture: `shares_outstanding` at 2023-12-31 handed a count
from a cover page nineteen months later, 60,500,000 against the filer's own
60,000,000 either side of it. The absence that stands in its place is one M5
shows with its reason, and the two are not mistakable for each other.

Where this sits against the two tasks ahead of it. M4-17 answers which facts
answer a period inside one filing and M4-18 settles one filing's eligible rules
into a state, both asked about a filing the caller names. This diff is what names
them, and what an entry is admitted with. Neither of those answers changes here
and neither of their tests is rewritten to make this pass; if the two cannot both
hold, that is a seam worth stopping on rather than a test worth editing.

Not in this diff, and the reason it stops here. Which of several answering
filings sets the value is the record's Rule 3, and the record states it twice in
ways that do not agree: the rule takes the filing with the greatest `filed` and
names it, while the restatement fixture the same record asks for expects the
values that did not change to go on naming the earlier filing. That is a
question for the decider and not for a run, so nothing here orders two filings,
parses `filed`, or reads a form. Which periods a filer has is Rule 1, and it
asks the settled answer, so it waits on the same ruling.
