GOALS.md's fifth M5 criterion names the dividend "growth rate and streak",
and its seventh says "Every free parameter arrives as a setting. Every
default states its reason." `docs/adr/dividend-growth-rate.md`, accepted on
2026-10-22, fixes the rate to the last step: the compound rate over n years
read at the computing year and the base year alone, three operations from
the arithmetic record's table, two conditions and their order, `-1` at a
computing-year zero, and how absence composes over two reads. The series
record fixes n as the setting `dividend_growth_years` with its preset and
reason, and says the setting arrives with the metric. The record's proof
section works every figure above by hand, and this task brings them; the
planner reproduced each in this run.

Why after M5-47 and not beside it. M5-46 and M5-47 pin a five-setting list
in every per-metric test file, and this task adds a sixth field, so the two
cannot run together; the decider said as much on #381. Declaring the field
last leaves the five pinned in their order and appends one.

Why the rate before the Piotroski tasks that follow. It is one metric with
one input, fully worked in its record, and it carries the setting whose
preset `discounted_cash_flow_years`' reason already refers to. The tasks
after it then pin the six-setting list once, in their new test files, and
never re-pin.

It depends on M5-47 because both own `crates/analyze/`, and because this
task re-pins the settings list that task leaves.

If the metric cannot be built without a seventh reason, a change to a
contract, to `method.rs`, to the entry shape M5-18 fixed, to the arithmetic
record's table or to anything M5-45 to M5-47 defined, if the setting cannot
be carried through the premises as the analyze record states, if the series
cannot be stopped at the gap without a change to `year.rs` that alters what
M5-36 proved, or if the source on reading says otherwise than the record,
that is an escalation.
