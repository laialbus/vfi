`docs/adr/how-analyze-computes-and-writes-a-figure.md`, accepted on
2026-10-07, fixes the two things the accepted analyze record handed "to the
first metric task, under the method version": the arithmetic analyze computes
in, and how a figure is written. GOALS.md's fourth, fifth, sixth, seventh and
ninth M5 criteria each need a figure computed and written, and the record's
two constants are the first entries in `constants/` under the sixth. This
task builds exactly what the record decides and decides nothing it leaves
open. The record separates "the implementing task" from "the first metric
that reads an amount", so the module lands before any metric and no metric
is computed here.

It depends on M5-15 because the skeleton it extends, `method.rs`, the empty
`settings/` and the entry point, must be on main, because `constants/` is
"created by the first method that has one" and the figure's two constants
are that, and because both own `crates/analyze/`. Through M5-15 it also
waits on the `literals` gate, so the two constants land under the gate that
reads them.

The record says the type's digits are held one per place in `RADIX` and
admits no wider limb; how the fractions are reduced is the task's, and the
record says speed rests on it. `vfi-analyze` takes no dependency for any of
this.

If the derivation needs a number the record's table under "Every number the
arithmetic itself needs" does not hold, a third allowlist value, a wider
limb, or an operation outside the table, that is an escalation, not a
literal and not a dependency.
