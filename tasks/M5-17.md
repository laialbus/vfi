`docs/adr/how-analyze-computes-and-writes-a-figure.md`, proposed by M5-16
and accepted by the decider in its fifth 2026-10-07 sweep, decides the two
things the accepted analyze record hands "to the first metric task, under
the method version": the arithmetic and the written form. It separates the
arithmetic's own proof from the first metric's two tests, so this task
builds the arithmetic alone and the first metric task reads amounts through
it. GOALS.md's fourth through seventh and ninth M5 criteria each need a
figure computed and written, and this is where every one of them will be.

It depends on M5-15 because the module lands beside `method.rs` and
`settings/`, under the `literals` gate that reads the one `constants/`
place, and because both own `crates/analyze/`.

The record fixes every number the arithmetic needs: zero and one from the
allowlist, `RADIX` and `SIGNIFICANT_DIGITS` from `constants/figure.rs`, a
tie decided by comparing a remainder against the divisor less itself, and
no half, no two and no limb width. A number the record's table does not
hold, or an operation outside its list, is an escalation, not a literal.

A root is the one operation that rounds inside the derivation. How the
implementing task keeps denominators small along a chain is its own, and
the benchmark holds whatever it chooses.
