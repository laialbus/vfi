GOALS.md's second M5 criterion asks that valuing a shortlist fit inside a
free-tier key's published limits. `docs/adr/price-allowance-between-sessions.md`,
accepted on 2026-10-04, decides where the record of what was spent lives
between sessions: a `SpentRecord` trait in `crates/fetch/src/price/spent/`,
a journal that appends one entry per request at a path it is handed, an
in-memory record for tests, and what one entry carries. This task builds
the interface, the entry and the two implementations exactly as that record
states them, and nothing the provider does with them. The ask order, the
two trailing windows, the pace seeded from the newest entry and the wall
clock handed in are the provider's, and belong to its task.

It depends on M5-24 because `crates/fetch/src/price/` is a key's place the
`secrets` gate reads, and AGENTS.md makes a change with no gate an
escalation; `price/spent/` is the one directory under it where filesystem
names may stand, and the gate's accept copy already carries the shape this
journal takes. The provider's own task still waits on a Tiingo key and on
`api.tiingo.com` in the sandbox's allowed domains, which hold neither; this
is the part of the second criterion that waits on neither.

The ledger's journal is the precedent for the layout, in the same crate,
and the record names its moment spelling as the one to follow. Whether a
line is framed as `written.rs` frames one, and how a moment is typed, is
the task's to decide inside the crate; what is fixed is the three fields,
the host check at the open and the three properties above.

If the journal cannot be written without naming `ApiKey` under `price/`,
without a dependency, or without something the record decided changing,
that is an escalation.
