Nothing runs over a filer's history end to end through `vfi-normalize` yet,
and the crate's own header says why: which periods a filer has and which of
several answering filings sets the value are the two rules it does not carry.
Both are now decided on record — Rule 3 by the accepted
`docs/adr/which-filing-sets-the-value.md`, and the case that record left open
by the record M4-30 proposes — so the task that wires the stage is the next
thing in the way of the golden fixtures the milestone names. Before it can be
queued, one thing it would otherwise have to invent must be decided.

The published vocabulary carries one state per concept per filer and no
period, and its `Value` asks for a source tag and a filing that an asserted
value and a silence-supplied zero do not have. M4-18 built the state to carry
what set it instead — a rule over named facts, an assertion, or the silence
reading — and left crossing that into the published shape to the wiring task,
rightly, because the published shape is a contract and a contract is above a
worker. The 2026-09-09 decider sweep asked for exactly this: one Structural
record, ahead of the wiring task, deciding the per-period shape and what those
two fields hold in those two cases. This is that record.

It follows M4-30 because that record may name a vocabulary version bump it
declines to take on its own, and a vocabulary that moves twice in one
milestone for two records written in parallel is the collision the queue
exists to prevent. What this record decides about a silence-supplied value is
how the surface states what the accepted Rule 3 record already says it
carries; which attempt's silence value stands against a read figure is M4-30's
and is read here, not reargued.

Nothing implements it. Not in this diff: Rule 3 and Rule 1 in code, the wiring
itself, and the normalize fixtures — each of which the planner queues once this
record and M4-30's are accepted, and each of which depends on this one.
