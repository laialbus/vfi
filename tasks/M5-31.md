GOALS.md's fourth M5 criterion names returns on capital, and the accepted
period record fixes the balance reading for all five of them: return on
assets and asset turnover (M5-29), return on equity (M5-30), and return on
capital employed and on invested capital, which are held here. They are
held because their denominators are terms no record forms.
`docs/adr/canonical-concepts.md` names `current_liabilities` as "the
capital employed denominator" and says nothing of the minuend; it names
`shareholders_equity` as "invested capital" and names no debt as a
component, though every source that defines invested capital includes
one; and it names an effective tax rate from `pretax_income` and
`income_tax_expense` without saying what that rate is where pretax income
is zero or negative or the tax line is a benefit, each of which a filing in
the corpus will present.

Why a record, and not the two metrics' code. Each of those is a method
choice with a source that the sources make differently: capital employed
as assets less current liabilities or as debt plus equity; invested capital
with or without cash netted, operating or financing side; the tax rate
effective, statutory or bounded. Two metric tasks choosing alone would be
two readings of terms that enterprise value and the discounted cash flow
bridge will read again, which anchor 5 and the one-source-of-truth
invariant forbid, and a worker that reads a debt concept the catalogue
does not name it a consumer of would be adding a consumer no record names.
One record, as `common-shareholder-forms.md` was for the common terms, is
where that is settled or found to need the catalogue's record.

It depends on nothing because everything it reads is on main: the catalogue
and its open-questions record, the two accepted analyze records, the
accepted period record, and the frozen contracts. The two metrics are
queued under this record's acceptance, not here.

If the record finds the terms cannot be stated without a seventh reason, a
change to a frozen contract's bytes, or a widening of the lint, it says so
by name as the acceptance above provides. If it cannot be written at all
without an accepted record changing, that is an escalation.
