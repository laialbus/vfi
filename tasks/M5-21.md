GOALS.md's fourth M5 criterion names liquidity. `docs/adr/canonical-concepts.md`
names these three as the consumers their concepts were admitted for:
`current_assets` and `current_liabilities` are "consumed by current, quick
and cash ratios" among others, `inventory` by "the quick ratio" and nothing
else, and `cash_and_equivalents` by "the cash ratio" among others. The
open-questions record settles what the cash ratio reads: "the cash ratio
consumes the narrow figure and net debt consumes both", so
`short_term_investments` stays out of it. All four concepts are balances at
an instant, so each metric reads one row and nothing across periods, and
none has a constant, a setting or a price. The difference is the first
subtraction in the derivation, and the arithmetic M5-17 builds makes it
exact.

Under `canonical-concepts` v2, `current_assets`, `current_liabilities` and
`inventory` apply to an operating filer alone, so a bank, an insurer, a
REIT and an investment company carry all three ratios as absent with
`input_not_applicable`, one reason per such input, which is the correct
absence and not a failure. `cash_and_equivalents` applies to every kind, so
for those filers `cash_ratio` names only `current_liabilities`.

It depends on M5-20 because each metric adds an entry of the shape M5-18
fixed, and because every metric task so far owns `crates/analyze/`, so
they are ordered rather than run together.

If the hand-over cannot carry these without a change to a contract, to
`method.rs`, to `settings/`, to what M5-17 defines or to the entry shape
M5-18 fixed, that is an escalation.
