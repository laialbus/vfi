`crates/normalize/src/lib.rs` says what is missing: "Nothing runs over a
filer's history end to end through them yet", and names the two rules of the
alignment ruleset the crate does not carry. This is the first of them. Both are
decided — Rule 3 by the accepted `docs/adr/which-filing-sets-the-value.md`,
which supersedes Rule 3 of `docs/adr/period-alignment.md` and nothing else of
it, and the silence case that record left open by
`docs/adr/silence-beside-a-read-figure.md`, accepted on 2026-09-15. The
`filings` module already says so in its header: "nothing here orders two
filings, parses `filed`, or reads a form", because when it landed the rule
was stated twice in ways that did not agree. It is stated once now, and this
task is the one that orders them.

The two records are one clause apart and land together on purpose. Rule 3
weighs the attempts of the filings that answer a period; the silence record
decides which of those attempts is a `Value` before Rule 3 sees it, because
the vocabulary turns silence into a zero for three concepts and a later thin
filing's zero would otherwise displace an earlier filing's read figure. Split
across two runs, the first would land a rule that erases a figure the filer
never took back.

Nothing here decides anything. Both records state their rules whole and this
task transcribes them; where the code would need a clause neither states — a
window, a day count, a quorum, a preference for an annual report over an
interim one, or any comparison of two figures — the record is wrong and the run
stops rather than inventing one. That is the failure mode the milestone names:
in normalization a plausible-looking wrong answer is what passes a glance.

Not in this diff: which periods a filer has, which is Rule 1 and asks this
settled answer, queued as M4-36 behind this; the published shape the stage
hands analyze, which is a contract version and is M4-37; the wiring of the
stage end to end and the six normalize golden fixtures, which wait on all
three.
