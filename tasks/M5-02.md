acceptance:
  - The deliverable is an ADR in the shape `docs/adr/TEMPLATE.md` sets, filled
    down to Consequences, with Status Proposed and Decision review left for
    the decider.
  - It names the first user-keyed price source. It cites the page where that
    source publishes its free-tier limits, per minute and per day and anything
    else a client must respect, and it gives the date the page was read. A
    limit recalled rather than read is the defect this criterion exists to
    prevent.
  - It names which prices M5's metrics consume, derived from the metrics
    GOALS.md names for M5 and the consumers `docs/adr/canonical-concepts.md`
    lists. That means which date or dates each metric needs a price at, and
    which field. It says whether a price is adjusted for splits or dividends,
    and why. A per-share figure from a filing divided by a price adjusted on
    another basis is a wrong number that looks right. A price no metric
    consumes is left out.
  - It states the provider interface, meaning what a caller asks for and what
    it gets back. What comes back is a price carrying the date it is for and
    where it came from, or an absence carrying its reason. A missing price is
    a state, not an error, as ANCHORS.md requires. There is one implementation
    per source behind the interface.
  - It states how the key reaches the engine, as a parameter and in no other
    way, never read by the engine from a keychain, file or the environment.
    How the shell stores it is the shell's business and is not decided here.
    With no key, every request is an absence with a reason and nothing fails.
  - It places the interface and the first implementation, naming the crate,
    the dependency edges `scripts/gates.sh`'s `allowed_edges` would need, and
    any layout change. It says how requests leave through the fetch stage's
    egress chokepoint, its host list and a rate limit named once with its
    citation, as EDGAR's requests do.
  - It shows the second M5 criterion by arithmetic from the cited limits.
    Screening the corpus makes no price call. It states how many calls valuing
    one company costs, and so how large a shortlist one day's allowance
    values. It also states what the provider returns once the allowance is
    spent, which is an absence with a reason and not a failure.
  - How a price reaches analyze, and what a price-dependent metric does when
    the price is absent, belong to M5-03's record and are not decided here.
  - It states its authority tier and what it touches, and it argues at least
    one other source as an alternative, with why not.
  - Nothing is implemented. The diff adds the record and nothing else, with no
    crate, manifest, lockfile, host list or code change.
---

GOALS.md's first two M5 criteria. ANCHORS.md fixes three things: prices sit
behind a provider trait with one implementation per source, the key is the
user's own and reaches the engine only as a parameter, and a missing price is a
state and not an error. Which source comes first, where the trait lives and
what exactly it returns are open, and they are a new interface and a new
placement. Both are above a worker.

`docs/adr/canonical-concepts.md` already keeps price and market capitalisation
out of the vocabulary and names the metrics that consume price through
`shares_outstanding` and `earnings_per_share_diluted`. Read the price's
consumers there rather than re-deriving them.

Read the source's limits from the source. If the sandbox cannot reach the page,
that is a stop and not a reason to recall the number.
