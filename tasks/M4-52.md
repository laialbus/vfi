The accepted `the-harness-checks-the-claims.md` decides what the golden
harness reads in a fixture's `claims` and how the files on main come into
that form. It names this task: exclusive, owning `golden.rs`, the module only
it declares, `scripts/gates.sh` through its owns, and the `claims` files. Until
it lands, the decider re-derives every claim by hand. After it, the decider
still re-derives the prose claims the record lists as unreadable to the
check.

Exclusive because it edits the gate runner, a protected path granted through
this file, and the `claims` files of five fixture directories. No task in
flight owns a `fixtures/normalize/` directory: M4-46 and M4-48 have merged,
and their files are among the five.

Not in this diff: any count, absence or `from:` derivation the check cannot
read, a structured claims syntax, and the `MembersEquity` registry question
the 2026-09-27 decider flagged.
