metrics, and its ninth says a metric with no price data is "absent with a
reason" and "never shows a wrong number".

2026-11-08, fixes price/free cash flow as the record's own form, market
capitalisation over the free cash flow

once, over a year, with its four conditions and their order and the same
exposure price/sales pins. It is the last of the record's four, and the
third reader of the free cash flow module, which that record named for it:
"the price/free-cash-flow multiple when it is built".

Why last. It composes two terms that are each already formed and proved,
market capitalisation averaged as M5-64 reads it and free cash flow as
M5-45 formed it, so nothing here is new but the composition and the two
conditions.

It depends on M5-64 because it reads the averaged term exactly as that task
reads it, and because both own `crates/analyze/`.

price/sales', and not this task's to close. This task pins the exposure, as
the record asks, and changes neither sentence.
