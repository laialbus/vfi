The milestone names negative equity among the golden fixtures it has to
produce, and no filer in the tree has it: the first facts filer's equity is
positive throughout, and the second's is not the case it was recorded for. A
fixture's expected result is read off the ruleset, and the ruleset is not
settled — Rule 3 waits on a human — but the data it will be read off has to
exist first, and a recording is the one part of a golden fixture that does not
wait.

The vocabulary already says what the case pins. `shareholders_equity` is a
balance whose sign is carried as presented, and its own note is the fixture's
sentence: negative when liabilities exceed assets, which is a value. What a
fixture guards against is the number being clamped, dropped, or read as an
absence somewhere downstream, so the recording has to show the sign in the
filer's own facts under the one element the committed registry reaches the
concept by. A filer whose deficit sits under some other element would resolve
`Unknown` and pin nothing about the sign.

This follows M4-25 rather than running beside it. A facts case at v2 holds the
filer's submissions document as well, since `report_period_end` is joined from
it, and a case recorded at v1's shape would be re-recorded the day v2 landed.
And M4-25 is what lets the filer be any filer: until it lands, nineteen in
twenty are refused at the parse, which is the constraint M4-20 had to search
under. The recording goes out through the stage's own recorder, with the
sandbox off for those commands and nothing else, as M4-20's did.

Not in this diff: anything that reads the case, and any kind for the filer,
which is asserted against its statements in a task of its own as M4-21 and
M4-22 are. M4-28 and M4-29 record the other two filers the milestone names,
and follow this one only because each writes the same harness header.
