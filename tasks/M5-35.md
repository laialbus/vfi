GOALS.md's fifth M5 criterion names sustainable growth.
`docs/adr/common-shareholder-forms.md`, accepted on 2026-10-14, defines it
on the common claim as `return_on_common_equity` times `retention_per_share`,
built on two metrics and forming no term, its reasons theirs in that order
and its conditions theirs. It is the first metric of the method derived from
other metrics' numbers rather than from concepts alone, and the record
leaves it no choice but its code.

It depends on M5-34 because it reads both the common return on equity M5-33
builds and the retention M5-34 builds, and because all three own
`crates/analyze/`.

If the metric cannot be built without a seventh reason, a change to a
contract, to `method.rs`, to `settings/` or to the entry shape M5-18 fixed,
that is an escalation.
