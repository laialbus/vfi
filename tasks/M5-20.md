GOALS.md's fourth M5 criterion names leverage. `docs/adr/canonical-concepts.md`
names these two as the consumers their concepts were admitted for:
`total_liabilities` is "consumed by liabilities to assets", `total_assets`
by "liabilities to assets" among others, `short_term_debt` and
`long_term_debt` by "total debt in debt/equity", and `shareholders_equity`
by "debt/equity". The open-questions record forms common equity as
`shareholders_equity` less `preferred_equity` only for the
"common-shareholder forms of return on equity, price/book and sustainable
growth"; debt/equity is not among them, so its denominator is
`shareholders_equity` as v2 carries it. All five concepts are balances at an
instant and apply to every kind, so each metric reads one row and nothing
across periods, and none has a constant, a setting or a price.

Two things are first here, and both stay inside the crate. These are the
first metrics stated at an instant period, where M5-18 and M5-19 leave the
results empty. And the sum of the two debts is the first composition of
inputs; how absence composes through it is already fixed by the accepted
analyze record, which this task applies and does not extend.

It depends on M5-19 because each metric adds an entry of the shape M5-18
fixed, and because every metric task so far owns `crates/analyze/`, so
they are ordered rather than run together.

A silence reading sets `short_term_debt` and `long_term_debt` to zero, so a
filer that presents no borrowing has a debt to equity of zero, which is a
figure and not an absence. If the hand-over cannot carry these without a
change to a contract, to `method.rs`, to `settings/`, to what M5-17 defines
or to the entry shape M5-18 fixed, that is an escalation.
