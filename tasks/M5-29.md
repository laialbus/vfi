GOALS.md's fourth M5 criterion names returns on capital, and
`docs/adr/canonical-concepts.md` names return on assets and asset turnover
as the consumers `total_assets` was admitted for, beside `net_income` and
`revenue`. Both pair a flow at a duration with a balance at an instant, so
each is the first kind of metric to read a second row, and
`docs/adr/which-other-period-a-metric-reads.md`, accepted on 2026-10-14,
is the rule it reads under: stated at the duration, the balance averaged
over the instants the duration opens and closes at, the two seeks by
equality of characters, two `sought_by` names, and absence in read order.
The record names "the task that implements the rule, alone or with the
first metric that reads it", and lists the proof it brings. This task is
that one, with the two metrics that share one denominator.

Why these two first. They read `total_assets`, which applies to every
kind, so the rule is proved on a balance no filer carries as
`NotApplicable`, and they need no constant, no setting and no price. The
other returns on capital wait: return on equity on M5-30, after this one,
and return on capital employed and on invested capital on the record M5-31
proposes for their terms.

It depends on M5-28 because the opening seek reads the start through
`date.rs` and names `start_not_a_date`, and because both own
`crates/analyze/`.

If the rule cannot be built without a seventh reason, a change to a
contract, to `method.rs`, to `settings/` or to the entry shape M5-18 fixed,
that is an escalation.
