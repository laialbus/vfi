GOALS.md's fourth M5 criterion names returns on capital.
`docs/adr/capital-employed-invested-capital-and-nopat.md`, accepted on
2026-10-16, forms invested capital once as `shareholders_equity` plus net
debt, the effective tax rate as `income_tax_expense` over `pretax_income`,
NOPAT as `operating_income` times one less that rate, and decides every
edge of the rate, declining rather than substituting at each; it names
return on invested capital as NOPAT over the average invested capital,
after tax, with the one exception the accepted records allow applied as
accepted. The period record names the metric averaged. Its proof section
works every figure above by hand, and this task brings them.

Why after M5-38. The module and its first two terms are M5-38's, and this
task adds the remaining four to the same file, so each is still formed
once. Return on invested capital is the larger metric by far, eight
concepts at thirteen reads with four conditions, and the first that reads a
term contributing nothing, which the reading has not carried before; that
is why it has a run of its own.

Why the sources read so. Under `sources-a-task-can-read.md`, Damodaran's
paper is open from the author's own site and is cited by the passages the
planner read; the CFA text's chapter and the Codification paragraph are
closed and named as such, and Regulation S-X stands beside them as the
open primary. The record itself flagged whether Damodaran nets marketable
securities as the item no check can see; the planner found the copy nets
cash and is silent on them, and the task says what to do with that.

It depends on M5-38 because it adds to the module that task creates and
reads the total debt it forms, and because both own `crates/analyze/`.

If the metric cannot be built without a seventh reason, a change to a
contract, to `method.rs`, to `settings/` or to the entry shape M5-18 fixed,
if the exception cannot be carried without changing another metric's reads,
or if a source on reading says otherwise than the record, that is an
escalation.
