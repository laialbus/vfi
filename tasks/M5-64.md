GOALS.md's fourth M5 criterion names "valuation multiples" among the value
metrics, and its ninth says a metric with no price data is "absent with a
reason" and "never shows a wrong number".
`docs/adr/market-capitalisation-and-the-equity-multiples.md`, accepted on
2026-11-08, fixes price/sales as the chapter's form over a year, reads the
count averaged under the period record's rule as written, asks the
share-basis rule once with E the end of Y, fixes its four conditions and
their order, and names the one exposure that reading leaves, the opening
count a split before E can put on another basis, asking that it be pinned
and not closed.

Why price/sales before price/free cash flow. It is the first metric to read
market capitalisation at two instants and average the term, so the averaged
reading of the module M5-63 built is proved here, on a denominator that is
one concept read at one row. Price/free cash flow, whose denominator is a
term of its own, is the next task's.

It depends on M5-63 because it reads the term that task forms and the
`non_positive_shares_outstanding` defined beside it, and because both own
`crates/analyze/`.

The two questions the record leaves open are this metric's cost and not this
task's to close: the averaged count is the period record's sentence read as
written, and the opening count the rule does not see is the share-basis
record's E for a metric stated at a duration. Each is a superseding record
of its own. This task pins the exposure, as the record asks, and changes
neither sentence.
