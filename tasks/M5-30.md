GOALS.md's fourth M5 criterion names returns on capital, and
`docs/adr/canonical-concepts.md` names return on equity as the consumer
`shareholders_equity` was admitted for, beside `net_income`. The accepted
period record fixes its balance reading, "return on equity, and on common
equity: averaged", so once M5-29 has built the seeks and the averaged read
this metric adds one entry and one quotient over them.

Why the total form alone. The open-questions record admits
`preferred_equity` and `preferred_dividends` for the common form, and the
record that forms common equity and income available to common,
`docs/adr/common-shareholder-forms.md`, is Proposed and not Accepted on
main. That record, as written, names the total form `return_on_equity`
with the preferred claim included and keeps it a metric of the method
beside the common one, so building the total form now reads nothing that
record decides and forms no term it owns. The common form, retention and
sustainable growth are queued under that record's acceptance, not here.

It depends on M5-29 because it reads through the seeks and the averaged
read that task builds, and because both own `crates/analyze/`.

If the metric cannot be built without a seventh reason, a change to a
contract, to `method.rs`, to `settings/` or to the entry shape M5-18 fixed,
that is an escalation.
