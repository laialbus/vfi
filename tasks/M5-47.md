GOALS.md's fourth M5 criterion ends with "discounted cash flow with margin
of safety". `docs/adr/discounted-cash-flow-and-margin-of-safety.md`,
accepted on 2026-10-19, fixes the derivation to the last step: eight steps
over the arithmetic record's operations, a growing perpetuity as the
terminal value, the bridge through net debt averaged, the per-share step
over the diluted count, three `declined` conditions, and how absence
composes. Its proof section works every figure above by hand, and this task
brings them. The margin of safety, built on this value, is the first metric
to read a price and waits on the record M5-48 proposes; the value per share
reads none, so a user with no key sees it today.

Why the value before the margin. The record says "The value per share is
computed where the margin is absent." It reads no price, so it needs no
crossing, no share-basis rule and no answer to the ordering question the
record leaves open. It does need the free cash flow module and the four
settings, which M5-45 and M5-46 build, and the net debt M5-41 formed.

It depends on M5-46 because it reads the settings that task defines, the
module M5-45 built, and because all three own `crates/analyze/`.

If the metric cannot be built without a seventh reason, a change to a
contract, to `method.rs`, to the entry shape M5-18 fixed, or to anything
M5-45 or M5-46 defined, if a step needs an operation outside the
arithmetic record's table, or if a source on reading says otherwise than
the record, that is an escalation.
