GOALS.md's fourth M5 criterion names "valuation multiples" among the value
metrics, and every record around them is accepted while none forms them: the
catalogue names each multiple's inputs and consumers, the provider record
lists each under the valuation date, the analyze record puts market
capitalisation and everything built on it under the share-basis rule through
`shares_outstanding`, the common-shareholder record fixes price/book's name
and its common form and leaves negative book and non-positive earnings to
the metrics' own records, and the discounted cash flow record forms the free
cash flow that price/free cash flow reads. The forms, the instant a count is
read at, the conditions and three of the names have no record.

Why a record, and not the metrics' tasks. Market capitalisation is one term
that four metrics here, enterprise value and Altman's X₄ will all read, so
it is formed once and reached through one module, as net debt and free cash
flow are, and what it is and where it lives is a decision above a worker. A
count at an instant beside a flow over a year puts the period record's
averaging rule and the share-basis rule's E in question for the first time,
and two accepted records leave the conditions at a negative book and a
non-positive earnings to this record by name. Each is a reading a worker
must not guess. One record settles them with their reasons, as the
discounted cash flow record settled its own.

Why now. Earlier plans held every price-dependent metric for the share-basis
rule, which is on main, and the last plan held the multiples for a record
and for a source, the CFA ratio list carrying no price ratio. Three chapters
of the origin, each stating a form in words, opened in this run from the
author's own site, where the discounted cash flow and dividend discount
records already read him.

Why enterprise value is left out. Whether it counts the preferred claim or
non-controlling interests is "its own record" by the common-shareholder
record's words, and which instant net debt is read at is open in the
discounted cash flow record. Both are that record's to settle on this one's
market capitalisation, once accepted.

It depends on nothing because everything it reads is on main. It owns one
new file, so it runs beside M5-53, M5-54 and M5-55 on `crates/analyze/`. The
metrics are queued under the record's acceptance, not here.

If the record finds a form cannot be stated without an operation outside
the arithmetic record's table, a new reason, a change to a frozen contract's
bytes, a v3 concept or a `unit` the entry shape does not carry, it says so
by name as the acceptance above provides. If it cannot be written at all
without an accepted record changing, that is an escalation.
