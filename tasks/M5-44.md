GOALS.md's fourth M5 criterion names financial health and the established
screens. Every record around Piotroski's score is accepted: the catalogue
names the concept each signal reads, the open-questions record fixed
`net_income` on his definition and named his ΔLEVER as a mover, the period
record fixed the instant each of his terms reads, the series record built
the year before a year so that his five change signals can compare, the
provider record kept his nine off the price path, and the lint record placed
his count of nine. What no record forms is the signals themselves: each
comparison, the score as their sum, the conditions, and what a filer whose
shape has no gross profit or no current subtotal gets.

Why a record, and not the metric's task. Three planning runs held the
score for want of an open copy of the paper. This run read one, the
author's school's reissue, and found the copy does not say one thing
everywhere: its body scales asset turnover by the opening balance and its
variable table by the average; its variable table reads long-term debt
with the current portion, which no v2 concept carries alone. EQ_OFFER
names an event, an equity issue, that v2 carries only as a change in a
weighted share count. And whether a bank, which has no gross margin and no
current ratio, gets a score over seven signals or no score is a choice
about what the score means. Each of these is a decision the derivation
cannot make silently, so one record settles them with their reasons, as
`capital-employed-invested-capital-and-nopat.md` settled its terms.

Why the sources read so. Under `sources-a-task-can-read.md`, the origin is
the journal paper as the period record names it, and the locator is written
from the copy the planner opened, the Chicago Booth reissue, by the section
and table numbers that copy carries. The period record cited §2.1 for his
readings; in this copy the signals stand under §2.3, and the task says so
rather than letting the two citations disagree in silence.

It depends on nothing because everything it reads is on main. It owns one
new file, so it runs beside M5-41 on `crates/analyze/` and beside M5-42 and
M5-43, which own records of their own. The metrics are queued under the
record's acceptance, not here.

If the record finds a signal cannot be stated without an operation outside
the arithmetic record's table, a seventh reason, a change to a frozen
contract's bytes, a v3 concept, or an edit to an accepted record, it says
so by name as the acceptance above provides. If it cannot be written at all
without an accepted record changing, that is an escalation.
