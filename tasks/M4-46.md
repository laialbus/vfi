also the first fixture to reach a `NotApplicable`, as
`suspension-fixture-is-the-bank-fixture.md` decides. That record queued it

record.

superseded by the bank record and are not pinned.

`revenue` claim under `bank` where the acceptance read as asking it to stop.
The ruling above states that claim, so the claims and `expected` of #243 come
back as they are; the decider found every other claim and count holding.
