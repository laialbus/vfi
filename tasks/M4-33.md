M4-28 landed the fifth filer whose facts are in the tree, CIK 0001778784,
recorded for a filer that paid a dividend and then stopped — one of the golden
fixtures the milestone names. Two of the five have a kind, and a third has its
assertion queued as M4-31; this one has none. This is the task M4-21, M4-22 and
M4-31 are for the others, queued on its own rather than left to the fixture
that will be written against it, for the reason M4-22 gave: a kind is read off
the filer's balance sheet and income statement against the vocabulary's
clause, and a fixture's expected result is read off the ruleset, and the two
are decided against different evidence.

The fixture needs the kind settled first, and this fixture more than most. Rule
1 of the accepted `docs/adr/period-alignment.md` makes a filer's period set a
function of it — "a period is the filer's when at least one concept its kind
admits resolves to a `Value` at it" — and that record says of the suspension
fixture that it "fails if alignment is wrong: a missing period in the middle
of a paying history reads as a suspension." An expected result written for a
filer with no kind would be written against a period set that changes the day
one is asserted, and for this fixture a period that moves is the wrong answer
it exists to catch.

What is particular to this filer is in the tree already, and the argument is
the run's to make from it. The recording carries no fact under
`us-gaap:GrossProfit` and none under any of the four cost-of-revenue elements
the committed registry's difference forms for `gross_profit` subtract, and none
under `us-gaap:AssetsCurrent` or `us-gaap:LiabilitiesCurrent`. Which kind that
argues, read against the vocabulary's own clause for it and refused for each
other kind by a fact, is not asserted here; what it means is that once a kind
stands, `gross_profit` — the concept whose `applies_to` is `operating` alone —
is where a real filer reaches or does not reach the third state, one of the
three the milestone requires and one no test may reach by naming a kind for
itself.

Not in this diff: anything that reads the case through the stage, which waits
on the stage being wired; and any kind for the filer M4-29 records, which is
queued once that recording is merged.
