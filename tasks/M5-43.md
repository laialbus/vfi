GOALS.md's fourth M5 criterion ends with "discounted cash flow with margin
of safety". Every term around it is fixed and none of it is: the analyze
record names its settings by example and leaves them to the metric; the
catalogue names free cash flow, the per-share step and the bridge as
consumers; the capital record, accepted on 2026-10-16, forms the net debt
the bridge reads and names the bridge as its other consumer; and the
provider record names the margin of safety as the consumer of the close at
the valuation date, with the analyze record fixing how a price-dependent
metric reads the crossing and is absent without one. The form of the
derivation itself, its settings and their presets, has no record.

Why a record, and not the metric's task. A discounted cash flow is a
method with more free parameters than any metric built so far, and each
preset is an assumption anchor 5 says must state its reason. Free cash flow
is formed today inside the payout metric's file; the multiple and this
metric would read it again, which the one-source-of-truth invariant forbids
without a module. The bridge, the terminal value and the per-share step are
each a choice the sources make differently. And the margin of safety is the
first metric to read a price, so what it does with the crossing is read
again by every price-dependent metric after it. One record settles these
with their reasons, as the capital record settled its terms.

Why now, without a provider. The equity value per share reads no price,
and the analyze record fixes that a metric with no price is absent with a
reason and never fails the run. The record can be written, reviewed and
accepted on the records on main; the margin of safety's `no_price` tests
need no provider, since the crossing is a contract type a test builds.

It depends on nothing because everything it reads is on main. It owns one
new file, so it runs beside M5-40, M5-41 and M5-42. The metrics are queued
under the record's acceptance, not here. The dividend discount valuation,
which the provider record pairs with this margin of safety, is left to a
record of its own that reads this one and the dividend growth rate's.

If the record finds the derivation cannot be stated without an operation
outside the arithmetic record's table, a new reason, a change to a frozen
contract's bytes, or a v3 concept, it says so by name as the acceptance
above provides. If it cannot be written at all without an accepted record
changing, that is an escalation.
