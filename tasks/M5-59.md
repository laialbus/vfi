GOALS.md's fifth M5 criterion ends with "dividend-based valuation", and its
seventh says "Every free parameter arrives as a setting. Every default
states its reason." `docs/adr/dividend-discount-valuation-and-margin-of-safety.md`,
accepted on 2026-11-06, names the three free parameters of the derivation,
fixes each one's meaning, unit, domain and preset, and gives each preset's
reason, two from sources the planner opened in this run and one as the
record's own decision. `docs/adr/what-analyze-takes-and-returns.md`
(Accepted) places every setting in `crates/analyze/src/settings/` behind a
constructor that refuses and never clamps, and M5-46 and M5-49 built the
settings of the two methods before this one there.

Why a task of its own. The settings are read by both metrics that follow,
and adding three fields to `Settings` rewrites the pinned settings list in
every per-metric test file, twenty files on main, which is a diff a reviewer
wants alone; the decider asked for that split on #381. The derivations that
read them are the next two tasks'.

It depends on M5-55 because both own `crates/analyze/`, and the share-basis
chain M5-54 and M5-55 is in flight there. It reads nothing either task
builds.

If a setting cannot be carried through the premises as the analyze record
states, if a domain cannot be refused without a literal outside `settings/`,
if the base years cannot be held within `u16` as the record requires, or if
a source on reading gives another figure than the record states, that is an
escalation.
