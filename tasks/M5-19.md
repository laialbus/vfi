GOALS.md's fourth M5 criterion opens with profitability and goes on to
leverage and financial health. `docs/adr/canonical-concepts.md` names each
of these three as the consumer its concept was admitted for: `revenue` is
"consumed by gross, operating and net margin", `operating_income` by
"operating margin" and "interest coverage", `net_income` by "net margin",
and `interest_expense` by "interest coverage" and nothing else. All four
are flows at a duration period, so each metric reads one row and nothing
across periods, and none has a constant, a setting or a price. They are the
three quotients that run exactly the path M5-18 lays for gross margin.

It depends on M5-18 because each metric adds an entry of the shape that
task fixes inside the crate, and because both own `crates/analyze/`.

Under `canonical-concepts` v2, `operating_income` applies to an operating
filer and a REIT alone, so a bank, an insurer and an investment company
carry `operating_margin` and `interest_coverage` as absent with
`input_not_applicable`, which is the correct absence and not a failure.
`net_income`, `revenue` and `interest_expense` apply to every kind, so
`net_margin` is computed for all of them. `interest_expense` is a
magnitude, positive when it is a cost; a filer whose statement nets it and
cannot be decomposed carries it `Unknown`, and the coverage is then absent
with that reason rather than computed over a gross figure nobody stated.

If the hand-over cannot carry these without a change to a contract, to
`method.rs`, to `settings/`, to what M5-17 defines or to the entry shape
M5-18 fixed, that is an escalation.
