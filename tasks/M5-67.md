GOALS.md's fifth M5 criterion opens its list of dividend metrics with "yield
and relative yield". Every record around the yield is accepted and none
forms it: the catalogue names `dividends_declared_per_share` for it, the
provider record lists it under the valuation date and fixes that it reads
the filing's dividend and the unadjusted close, the common-shareholder
record lists it as common by the concept it reads, and the analyze record
puts it under the share-basis rule. Its period, its conditions, its name and
its source have no record.

Why a record, and not the metric's task. The name crosses `analyze-store`
v1 as characters; whether a negative figure declines or is a signed yield,
and whether a year or every duration is the period, are readings a worker
must not guess; and relative yield, when it has a record, sets this yield
against its own past, so the form it inherits is fixed once and above a
worker.

Why now. Earlier plans held the yield because no open text defined it: the
CFA ratio list carries no price ratio, and Weiss and Lowe is a closed book.
A chapter of the author the discounted cash flow, dividend discount and
market capitalisation records already read opened whole in this run from
his own site, and it states the form in one line.

Why relative yield is left out. No copy a run can open states it, the
provider record's sentence is all that is accepted of it, and its form would
be set on the yield this record has not yet fixed. It is a record of its
own, after this one.

It depends on nothing because every record it reads is accepted and on main,
`non_positive_close` and `negative_dividends_declared_per_share` stand on
main where the record reaches them, and M5-54's reading is merged. It owns
one new file, so it runs beside M5-63 to M5-65 on `crates/analyze/` and
beside M5-66. The metric is queued under the record's acceptance, not here.

If the record finds the form cannot be stated without an operation outside
the arithmetic record's table, a new reason, a change to a frozen contract's
bytes or a `unit` the entry shape does not carry, it says so by name as the
acceptance above provides. If it cannot be written at all without an
accepted record changing, that is an escalation.
