The milestone names a fiscal-year change among the golden fixtures it has to
produce, and `docs/adr/period-alignment.md` says plainly where that stands: "No
filer in the repository has one yet." The fixture that record describes must show
"the transition period present at its own length with its own two dates, the
years either side present at theirs, no period synthesised to fill the gap
between them, and the quarters before and after the change not colliding" — every
one of those a claim about a filer's own dates, so the data has to exist before
anything can be expected of it.

The fetch harness already says what a second facts case is for. The first,
`every-fact-a-filer-reported`, "is recorded here so the second is a different
case rather than a copy of this one", and "a second facts case earns its place by
differing in one of those — a filer that amended a filing, one that changed its
fiscal year, one whose history is decades rather than years." This takes the
middle one, because it is the case the milestone names that no filer in the tree
can serve.

The recording is live and goes out through the recorder under
`crates/fetch/src/bin`, for the reason the harness gives: what another client
receives and what this stage receives are not the same thing, so a case recorded
elsewhere pins that client and leaves the stage only assumed to agree. sec.gov is
not on the shell's allowlist, so that one command runs with the sandbox off, as
M3-10's and M4-07's did and for the same reason; nothing else in the run needs
it.

Not in this diff: anything that reads the case. The ruleset that would turn those
dates into a filer's periods is not landed, so there is no normalize fixture to
write yet and no expected result to state for one. What this leaves behind is the
recording that fixture will be written against.
