The stage retrieves company facts for about one filer in twenty. Escalation
`2026-09-08-companyfacts-cik-as-a-number.md`, carried in by M4-20, says why:
EDGAR spells the `cik` of a company facts document two ways — the ten-digit
string the submissions endpoint uses, and a bare number — and the reader types
the field as a string, so a document of the second kind is refused whole. Of the
217 filers the worker sampled, 207 are the second kind, Apple and Elanco among
them; the repository holds a filer whose history retrieves and whose facts do
not. Both facts fixtures are recordings of the minority, one by chance and one
by choice, so no gate sees it.

The worker did not fix it, and was right not to: `Filer.cik` is a contract
field, published at v1 as "the filer the document is about, as the document
states it", and what should cross when the document states `1753391` is a
decision. The per-filer registry binds a filer to its CIK "ten digits
left-padded with zeros", and normalize will key a filer by that string, so the
two spellings cannot both cross as published. The decider read the escalation
as Structural and recommended one record: fix `cik` as the key, folded into the
`fetch-normalize` v2 the accepted alignment record already proposes for
`report_period_end`, so that one version carries both fields and the contract
moves once. This is that record, proposed.

The alignment record's own proposal rides along for a reason beyond economy.
That record assigns the task publishing v2 one more thing — "recording a fixture
that holds" the claim that EDGAR's `reportDate` is the end of the period a
periodic filing reports, checked on nine filings of one filer — and the
2026-09-06 plan asked what such a fixture could check, since the facts document
publishes nothing about the period a filing is about. The record answers that
here, where both fields are being argued together, so the publishing task
inherits a check it can run rather than a claim it has to decide how to test.

Nothing implements it. Publishing v2, teaching the retrieval both spellings, and
recording a number-spelled filer — the fixture that would then pin the majority
case, and the first recording since M4-20 that need not filter on a spelling —
wait on this being accepted, and the escalation file waits with them: the change
that acts on the answer deletes it in the same diff. This depends on nothing in
the queue beside it: it reads a published surface, an accepted record, an
escalation and three fixtures, all on main, and touches no crate, no manifest,
no gate and no data.
