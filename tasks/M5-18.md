GOALS.md's fourth M5 criterion opens with profitability, and gross margin
is its first line: `canonical-concepts` v2 defines `gross_profit` as
revenue less the cost of producing what was sold, applying to operating
filers alone, and `revenue` for every kind. Both are flows at a duration
period, so the metric reads one row and nothing across periods. It has no
constant and no setting, and no price, so it is the smallest metric that
runs the whole path the accepted records fix: two inputs read through the
arithmetic (`how-analyze-computes-and-writes-a-figure.md`), one quotient,
one written figure, and every absence under the closed reason set
(`what-analyze-takes-and-returns.md`, `analyze-store` v1). It is also where
the record's two metric tests land, an unreadable amount and a zero
divisor.

It depends on M5-17 because it reads and writes through that module and
names the condition defined beside its reader, and because both own
`crates/analyze/`.

A bank, an insurer, a REIT and an investment company carry `gross_profit`
as `NotApplicable`, so for them the metric is absent with
`input_not_applicable`, which is the correct absence and not a failure. A
negative gross profit is a value and gives a negative figure.

The entry's shape is the one decision here above a single metric, and it
is inside the crate. If the hand-over cannot carry the metric without a
change to a contract, to `method.rs`, to `settings/` or to what M5-17
defines, that is an escalation.
