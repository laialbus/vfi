GOALS.md's fourth M5 criterion names profitability first among the value
metrics. `docs/adr/canonical-concepts.md` derives the vocabulary from the
metrics and names gross, operating and net margin as the consumers of
`revenue`, `gross_profit`, `operating_income` and `net_income`. They are the
first metrics because they read a flow against a flow at one period, so they
need no period pairing, no price, no constant and no setting, and they are
the first metric to read an amount, which the figure record says "adds two
tests": characters outside the grammar give `declined` with
`input_not_a_decimal`, and a zero divisor gives its own `declined`.

The accepted analyze record fixes the signature, the three places, the
closed reasons and how absence composes, and leaves "which periods a metric
reads" to the metric's task. A flow at an instant resolves `Unknown` under
`docs/adr/what-normalize-emits.md`, which says which rows mean anything for
a concept is M5's, so this task states the margins at duration periods only
rather than carrying three `input_unknown` absences at every balance date.
The two `operating`-only clauses, `gross_profit` and `operating_income`,
make `input_not_applicable` reachable from the history at a bank or an
insurer.

It depends on M5-17 for the arithmetic every figure goes through, and
because both own `crates/analyze/`. How a metric declares its inputs and
reaches the hand-over is this task's to shape; every later metric task
adds to that shape, so the file that lists the metrics is named for the
planner.

Returns on capital are not here: a balance beside a flow needs the
period-pairing rule and the average's cited `2`, which is a task of its own
after this one.

If a margin needs a literal the allowlist does not hold, a setting, a
reading under which a `NotApplicable` term contributes nothing, or a change
to anything M5-17 built, that is an escalation.
