  - The last of them is a check rather than a judgement, and is done as one: the

    `long_term_debt`, `dividends_declared_per_share` and `dividends_paid` — over

  - Where an answer adds or splits a concept, the record states it the way the
    vocabulary states one: meaning, flow or balance, unit and sign convention,
    the kinds it applies to, its silence reading, and the M5 metric that
    consumes it. A concept no metric consumes does not enter the set.
  - Where an answer changes what an existing concept means, the record names the
    concept, the change, and the consuming metric that moves with it.
  - `docs/adr/canonical-concepts.md` is not edited. It is accepted, and an
    accepted record is never edited; this one closes what it left open, in the
    way `fetch-normalize-contract.md` closed the eighth question.
  - Nothing is implemented: the diff adds the record and nothing else. No
    `contracts/` directory, no registry, no code under `crates/`.

The vocabulary is accepted and cannot publish. Its own decision says every open
question must close before v1, and that none of them closes by picking the
reading that unblocks the next task — three change numbers rather than names, so
a guess here is the failure mode M4 exists to prevent, arriving as a plausible
number nobody can trace.

They are one task rather than several because four of them are the same question
asked about different concepts — one concept with a wide meaning, or two
combined in analysis — and the vocabulary is where that shape has to be answered
consistently. Split across records, it can be answered one way for leases and
another for short-term investments, and nothing would catch it.

A contract between stages is above a worker, so this is a proposal. What it
unblocks is the publication of the vocabulary at v1, which is a separate task and
depends on this one.
