GOALS.md's fourth M5 criterion names "valuation multiples" among the value
metrics, and its ninth says a metric with no price data is "absent with a
reason" and "never shows a wrong number".
`docs/adr/market-capitalisation-and-the-equity-multiples.md`, accepted on
2026-11-08, fixes the four equity multiples to the last step. Price/earnings
is the one that reads no count and forms no term: the close over the diluted
figure, one division, three conditions and their order, and the price read
through M5-54's one function with E the end of Y.

Why this one first. It is the simplest of the four and the only one that
reads nothing the market capitalisation module will hold, so it brings the
multiples' shared shape, the year test, one read beside the price read, the
`non_positive_close` reached from the discounted cash flow margin, without
the term. The three that follow build on the term M5-63 forms.

It depends on M5-61 because both own `crates/analyze/`, and the dividend
discount chain M5-59 to M5-61 is in flight there. It reaches
`non_positive_close` as M5-61 leaves it.

If the metric cannot be built without a seventh reason, a change to a
contract, to `method.rs`, to the entry shape M5-18 fixed, to `settings/`, to
what M5-54 built, or to anything M5-45 to M5-61 defined beyond widening a
constant's visibility, if a case in the record's proof cannot be given the
outcome the record states, or if a source on reading says otherwise than
the record, that is an escalation.
