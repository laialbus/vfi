GOALS.md's fourth M5 criterion names returns on capital. Every one of them
divides a flow by a balance: `net_income` over `total_assets`, over
`shareholders_equity`; `operating_income` over capital employed. v2 states
each flow at a duration and each balance at an instant, a `Row` holds one
period, and so far every metric reads its own row and no other. The first
return on capital is the first metric to read a second row, and
`docs/adr/what-analyze-takes-and-returns.md` already gives it the reason it
needs when the row is not there: `no_period`, "naming the period the metric
was computing at and the name of the rule that sought the other period".
What that rule is, the record leaves to "that metric's task".

Why a record, and not the first such metric's code. Which balance a flow is
read against is a method choice the sources make differently: a closing
balance, an opening balance, or the two averaged, each defensible and each
a different number from the same history. Five metric tasks choosing alone
would be five readings of one rule, which is what anchor 5 and the
one-source-of-truth invariant forbid, and the 2026-10-09 and 2026-10-10
plans held these metrics for exactly this. One record before any of them,
as `how-analyze-computes-and-writes-a-figure.md` was for the arithmetic,
means every metric that reads across periods shares one rule and none of
them chooses. The same record is where the preceding-period reading that
Piotroski's deltas and the dividend series need is decided or named as
another record's, so that two records do not read a date two ways.

It depends on nothing because everything it reads is on main: the two
accepted analyze records, the frozen `canonical-concepts` v2 and
`analyze-store` v1, and the metric shape M5-18 fixed. The returns on
capital, asset turnover, sustainable growth, the screens and the scores are
queued under this record's acceptance, not here.

If the record finds the rule cannot be stated without a seventh reason, a
change to a frozen contract's bytes, or a widening of the lint, it says so
by name as the acceptance above provides. If it cannot be written at all
without an accepted record changing, that is an escalation.
