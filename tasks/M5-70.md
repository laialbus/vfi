GOALS.md's fourth M5 criterion names "valuation multiples" among the value
metrics, and its ninth says a metric with no price data is "absent with a
reason" and "never shows a wrong number".
`docs/adr/enterprise-value-and-its-multiples.md`, accepted on 2026-11-21 and
Accepted on main since #430, fixes the two multiples whose numerator is the
value of the firm: enterprise value as one addition over two terms main
already forms once, EV/EBIT as that term averaged over a year's operating
income, its name, its five conditions and their order, and a proof worked by
hand. This task has only its code left to decide.

Why the term and EV/EBIT together. The term has no reader until a multiple
reads it, and EV/EBIT reads it over a concept, with no second term to form,
so the module and its first reader are proved in one diff and the module
never stands with a `dead_code` expectation, as M5-63 built market
capitalisation with price/book. EV/EBITDA, which needs the EBITDA term
beside it, is the next task's.

Nothing the term needs is missing on main. `reads_with_nothing` already
reads net debt's four balances averaged with the one exception, as
`return_on_invested_capital` and the discounted cash flow bridge read them,
and `market_capitalisation` already gives the term at each instant the count
was read at.

It depends on M5-68 because both own `crates/analyze/`, because the name
lists it appends to are the ones M5-68 leaves, and because the arm is held
or reached as M5-68 leaves it.

The three questions the record leaves open, whether the period record's
averaging sentence reaches the balances of a value the close is set beside,
whether the share-basis rule sees a count read before E, and a concept for
non-controlling interests, are this metric's cost and not this task's to
close. This task pins the exposure, as the record asks, and changes no
sentence of an accepted record.

If the term cannot be formed once without a second product or a second net
debt elsewhere, if the metric cannot be built without a seventh reason, a
change to a contract, to `method.rs`, to the entry shape M5-18 fixed, to
`Unit`, to `settings/`, to what M5-54 or M5-63 built, to the capital module,
or to anything M5-45 to M5-68 defined beyond what the acceptance above
allows, if a case in the record's proof cannot be given the outcome the
record states, or if a source on reading says otherwise than the record,
that is an escalation.
