The burn this closes is on origin now: `escalation-M2-12` records a worker
that claimed M2-12, found its precondition unmet, escalated well, and
released the claim — putting the task straight back in the pool for the next
worker to stop on. A deliberate escalation means retrying reproduces the
wall, so the task must leave the pool until the escalation is resolved.

Presence parks, deletion restores: derived state, no status field, nothing
to go stale — the same reasoning the workplan gives for deriving claims from
branches. The one-sentence availability rule in the workplan gains its
parking clause by the human's hand; that file is protected and above this
task, so state the rule in escalations/README.md and leave the workplan
alone.
