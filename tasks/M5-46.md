GOALS.md's fourth M5 criterion names the discounted cash flow, and its
seventh says "Every free parameter arrives as a setting. Every default
states its reason." `docs/adr/discounted-cash-flow-and-margin-of-safety.md`,
accepted on 2026-10-19, names the four free parameters of the derivation,
fixes each one's meaning, unit, domain and preset, and gives each preset's
reason, two from sources the planner opened in this run and two as the
record's own decision. `docs/adr/what-analyze-takes-and-returns.md`
(Accepted) places every setting in `crates/analyze/src/settings/` behind a
constructor that refuses and never clamps, and M5-37 built the first one
there.

Why a task of its own. The settings are read by both metrics that follow,
and adding four fields to `Settings` rewrites the pinned settings list in
every per-metric test file, which is a diff a reviewer wants alone. The
derivation that reads them is the next task's.

It depends on M5-45 because both own `crates/analyze/`. It reads nothing
that task builds.

If a setting cannot be carried through the premises as the analyze record
states, if a domain cannot be refused without a literal outside `settings/`,
or if a source on reading gives another figure than the record states, that
is an escalation.
