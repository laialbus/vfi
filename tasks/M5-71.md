GOALS.md's fourth M5 criterion names "valuation multiples" among the value
metrics, and its ninth says a metric with no price data is "absent with a
reason" and "never shows a wrong number".
`docs/adr/enterprise-value-and-its-multiples.md`, accepted on 2026-11-21 and
Accepted on main since #430, fixes EV/EBITDA as the chapter's printed form:
the enterprise value M5-70 forms, over EBITDA, a second term formed once in
a module of its own, with its name, its five conditions and their order and
a proof worked by hand. It is the last of the record's two, and with it the
"valuation multiples" the catalogue names are all computed.

Why last. It composes the enterprise value term as M5-70 proves it with one
new addition, so nothing here is new but the EBITDA term, its two conditions
and the composition. `depreciation_and_amortization` gains its first
consumer: nothing under `crates/analyze/src/` reads it on main.

It depends on M5-70 because it reads the term that task builds, reaches
`non_positive_enterprise_value` where that task defines it, and because both
own `crates/analyze/`.

The three questions the record leaves open are this metric's cost as they
are EV/EBIT's, and not this task's to close.
