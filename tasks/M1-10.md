title: Sweep abandoned claims from origin
milestone: M1
owns:
  - scripts/tasks.sh
depends_on: []
exclusive: no
acceptance:
  - A tasks.sh verb releases abandoned claims — a branch on origin named for
    a task still in the queue, with no open PR, left behind by a run that is
    no longer alive. WORKPLAN.md's abandoned-claims rule states the outcome;
    the verb implements it.
  - It never releases a live run's claim. The rule that separates dead from
    live, and why it is safe, is stated where the verb implements it. If
    origin alone cannot distinguish them safely, the verb takes the
    conservative side and reports what it skipped rather than guessing.
  - Branches under escalated/ are never touched; they are records, not
    claims.
  - available and claim behave exactly as before.
---

Today a hard-killed run — power loss, SIGKILL — leaves its claim branch on
origin forever: the task looks taken, workers skip it, and the queue silently
shrinks until a human notices and deletes the branch by hand. The wrapper's
exit trap releases claims on every failure it survives; this verb is the
backstop for the failures it does not. Wiring it into a scheduled run is
wrapper-side (one line in run.sh, outside the workspace, installed by the
human); until then the verb is run by hand, which is still one command
instead of a hand-audit of origin.
