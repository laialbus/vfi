The milestone names a dividend suspension among the golden fixtures it has to
produce, and no filer in the tree pays a dividend. The two dividend concepts are
the ones the vocabulary could not let fall back to `Unknown`: a suspension has
to read as a zero or the streak cannot be measured, so both carry a conditional
silence reading — zero when the period's facts are silent on both concepts
alike — and that reading has never been exercised on a filer that stopped
paying rather than one that never started. This is the recording it will be
exercised on.

What the case has to show is in the filer's own facts and nowhere else: a run
of periods with a dividend under an element the registry lists, then periods
the filer reported in full with none. The second half is the part that needs
care. A thin comparative column is silent about most things, and reading its
silence as a suspension is exactly the mistake the alignment record warns
about — "a missing period in the middle of a paying history reads as a
suspension" — so the periods after the suspension have to be ones the filer
filed its own report of, which at v2 a filing's `report_period_end` says. The
alignment record asks whoever records this case to say which periods it
expects and not only which dividends, and the header is where that is said.

This follows M4-27 only because both write the harness header, and M4-25
because a facts case at v2 holds the submissions document too. Not in this
diff: anything that reads the case, and any kind for the filer, which is its
own task.
