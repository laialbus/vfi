---
id: M3-02
title: Requests stay inside the source's published rate limit
milestone: M3
owns:
  - crates/fetch/
  - Cargo.toml
  - Cargo.lock
depends_on: [M3-01]
exclusive: no
acceptance:
  - The rate the source publishes is named once, in one place, and carries the
    page that publishes it. An uncited number is the defect this criterion
    exists to prevent.
  - Requests cannot leave faster than that rate, and the bound holds across
    concurrent callers rather than within one sequence — the chokepoint is
    shared, and a limit counted per caller does not bound what the source sees.
  - Whatever else the published access policy requires of a client is sent on
    every request, sourced the same way. A value that is the user's to supply
    arrives as a parameter, never a literal in the engine.
  - The spacing is proved against a clock the test controls, not by sleeping. A
    proof that costs real seconds is a proof someone weakens later.
  - No test added here reaches the live network.
---

The limit and the client identification EDGAR asks for are published by the
source, so read them there rather than recalling them: a rate that is nearly
right reads exactly like one that is right until the source starts refusing.
Cite what you read.

This sits at the chokepoint M3-01 builds, so it lands after it and inside the
same crate.
