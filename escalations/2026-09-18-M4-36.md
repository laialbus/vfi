# M4-36 — Rule 1's count does not re-derive: every silence zero is a `Value`

**Doing.** Rule 1 of `docs/adr/period-alignment.md` over M4-35's
`standing::stands`. For each candidate period of CIK 0002003750, it asks every
concept the kind admits, with the cover-page entry never admitted. A period is
the filer's where one of them stands as a `Value`.

**What stopped it.** Read as written, the set is all 50 candidates (24
durations, 26 instants), not 32. The vocabulary's silence reading produces a
`Value` of zero with no entry and no fact behind it. `short_term_investments`
reads `Zero` outright, so it stands at 0 at every instant that some filing
answers. That includes the ten cover dates and the three lease instants. The two
dividend concepts read their conditional zero at every duration that some filing
answers, and that includes the lease terms and the calendar tax years. The
record says resolution "can only narrow that set further, never widen it". That
argument is about candidates found through tags. It does not cover step 4's
silence reading, which `docs/adr/silence-beside-a-read-figure.md` later carried
over the period. The task says to stop in this case and not restate the claim.

**The reading that re-derives, not taken.** Leave out a `Value` that
`SetBy::Silence` set, so that only values a rule or an assertion set count. That
gives exactly 32: 19 durations and 13 instants. The 18 left out are the strays
the record names: 2023-09-01..2024-11-30, 2024-09-01..2024-11-30,
2024-12-01..2025-11-30, 2024-01-01..2024-09-30, 2024-01-01..2024-12-31,
2023-09-01, and twelve instants from 2024-05-08 to 2026-08-13. Both named
periods, 2022-09-30 and 2022-10-01..2023-09-30, are in. The rule's words "through
entries that answer the period asked for" arguably exclude a value that no entry
set, but no record says so. Which values make a period exist is a normalization
rule, so it is not a worker's reading to choose.

**Needed to proceed.** A ruling, in a record or in the task, on whether a
silence zero counts toward Rule 1's "resolves to a `Value`". If it does not, the
task can proceed as written. The plumbing is small: `filings`/`standing` gain a
crate-private way to ask every filing with `Admits::OnlyThePeriodAskedFor`, and
nothing public changes.

**Also for the ruling.** The task wants a filer with no kind established to have
no periods, because no concept is one "its kind admits". `applicability::ask`
lets such a filer proceed on every concept, and unscoped registry entries still
resolve for it. The task's reading is workable, but the ruling should confirm it,
since it departs from how the rest of the crate treats `None`.
