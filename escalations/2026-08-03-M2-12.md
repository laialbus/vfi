drafting begins, and if it is not, stop rather than re-propose it.

Status: Proposed, Decided: —, and its Decision review section is unfilled.
It was proposed the same day by the supervised session that queued this
task; no open pull request accepts it (#52 is a decider sessions sweep).
Nothing outside the record accepts it either — acceptance is a status on the
record itself.

Accepting it is not this run's to do on either count. Proposer and decider
are never the same agent, and a worker is neither; and `docs/adr/` is outside
what M2-12 owns, which is `docs/proposals/` alone.

## The part that needs a decision, not just a nudge

The two ways this unblocks are not equivalent, and one of them is closed.

The ADR's authority is Structural, so the decider may accept it alone and it
stands, flagged for later human review. That path is open and unblocks M2-12
as written.

The other path the ADR names for itself is not. Its Decision review line
leaves acceptance "for the human, if acceptance is taken together with the
installation this ADR describes," and its Decision says installation is a
human act that this ADR's acceptance is the sign-off for. But the thing to be
installed is the draft M2-12 produces, and M2-12 may not begin until the ADR
is Accepted. Taken together: the draft waits on acceptance, acceptance waits
on installation, installation waits on the draft. Held to the letter, M2-12
can never start.

## What would let this proceed

Any one of these, in preference order:

1. The decider reviews the ADR and sets it Accepted. M2-12 is then claimable
   exactly as written, with nothing else changed.
2. A human accepts it, deferring only the installation. Same effect — the
   draft is written, the human installs it later against the accepted record.
3. If acceptance is genuinely meant to happen at install time, then M2-12's
   first criterion is the wrong gate and the planner should relax it to name
   the ADR as Proposed-and-not-Rejected. This is a task edit, so it is the
   planner's, not a worker's.

A rejection or a deferral also resolves this: M2-12 should then be withdrawn
from the queue rather than left to be claimed and stopped on again.
