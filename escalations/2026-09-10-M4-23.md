- Date: 2026-09-10
- Branch: M4-23

M4-23 asks for a record superseding Rule 3 of `docs/adr/period-alignment.md`, and
asks it to say what each fixture tied to that rule must show, "re-derived from
the merged fetch fixtures" — for the restatement, "`earnings_per_share_diluted`
and the nine agreeing concepts at the half-year 2023-10-01 to 2024-03-31 and the
quarter 2024-01-01 to 2024-03-31, each with the value and the accession it
names". The same criterion says that where a claim about a fixture does not
re-derive, the run stops rather than adjusting it. It does not re-derive.

Read off `fixtures/fetch/every-fact-a-filer-reported` against
`registry/concepts/` as it stands — 74 elements, the same set since #137, since
#155 added only `reading` and `answers`:

- **The half-year** is answered by two filings, 38 facts each. They carry
  **thirteen** mapped elements in common, not ten; twelve agree to the digit and
  `EarningsPerShareDiluted` differs, −0.002 against −0.0015. Twelve concepts
  resolve to a `Value` in each filing — revenue 264,956, gross_profit 103,206,
  operating_income −76,928, pretax_income −77,035, income_tax_expense 367,
  net_income −77,402, interest_expense 536, depreciation_and_amortization 401,
  operating_cash_flow −127,584, capital_expenditure 3,729,
  diluted_shares_weighted_average 50,301,639, and earnings_per_share_diluted. So
  **eleven agree, not nine**. `shares_outstanding` is not among them: it is a
  balance, and the cover-page entry, which the accepted record holds out at v1,
  is the only entry that would answer a duration at all.
- **The quarter** is answered by **four** filings, not two: the two above, and
  the 10-Qs `0001213900-24-067900` filed 2024-08-13 and `0001213900-25-078735`
  filed 2025-08-20, each carrying `NetIncomeLoss` −24,908 at it. The two full
  ones share nine mapped elements, eight agreeing; eight concepts resolve, seven
  agreeing. So the count is not the only thing off — the accepted record has the
  agreeing concepts "naming *that* accession", the 10-Q filed 2024-05-08, and
  for `net_income` at this quarter the greatest `filed` is a third filing that
  neither the record nor the task names.

One fact bounds whatever resolves this: **the two readings never disagree about
a number.** Both take the value of the answering filing with the greatest
`filed` — where the filings agree the number is the same either way — so what
the reading decides is only which accession, and with it which tag and which
rule, a `Value` names. Every difference between them is provenance.

To proceed I need one of two sentences. Either the criterion restated with the
counts that re-derive — twelve concepts at the half-year, eleven agreeing; eight
at the quarter, seven agreeing; four answering filings at the quarter — or a
ruling that the superseding record states the re-derived list and records that
the accepted record's ten elements and nine concepts are wrong. I did not take
either myself: the count is an accepted record's, accepted records are not
edited, and this task's own acceptance tells the run to stop rather than adjust
a fixture claim.
