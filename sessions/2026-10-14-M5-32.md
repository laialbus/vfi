# 2026-10-14 — M5-32

Branch `M5-32`. Proposes `docs/adr/which-duration-precedes-a-duration.md`. A
duration is a year from its dates alone: twelve months to a month's end or
52/53 weeks (26 U.S.C. §441), and no other year-length duration overlapping
it. The overlap clause keeps out the fixture's calendar 2023, which holds the
transition period's figures, so the year before the first new-calendar year is
`no_period`. The year before ends the day before the start; a series repeats
that seek. Settings `dividend_growth_years` (5) and `dividend_streak_years`
(10); the streak counts paying years. New: `sought_by` `preceding_year` and
`preceding_opening_instant`; conditions `end_not_a_date`, `not_a_year` and
`overlaps_another_year`; date ordering in `date.rs` for the overlap test only.
