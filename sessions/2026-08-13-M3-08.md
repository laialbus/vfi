# 2026-08-13 — M3-08

Branch `M3-08`. The funnel gains its third step and `funnel::run`, which is one
pass end to end: seed set, metadata gate, then a history for the filers the
ledger says the gate admitted and for nobody else. The retrieval is M3-03's; what
is new is the step boundary and the verdict. Every step of a pass is handed the
same set and reads out of the ledger which of them it is about, so no list of
survivors travels beside the record.

Three verdicts here: the history arrived, EDGAR publishes no filing for the filer
— an absence, not a failure — or the retrieval failed and nothing was judged. A
retrieved history goes to a sink the caller supplies, after its verdict is on
record and never before. Where it goes after that is the fetch → normalize
contract, still open.

The negative criterion is a case over the requests that left; a step that ran
over every judged filer instead turns it red. Two cases drive a pass against a
real journal — one killed part way, one complete after it — pinning that an
interrupted sweep keeps what it completed and is told from the next by its pass
number rather than its clock. `fixtures/fetch/a-filer-the-gate-admits` now pins
all three steps.
