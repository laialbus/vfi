# 2026-10-19 — M5-43

Branch `M5-43`. Proposes `docs/adr/discounted-cash-flow-and-margin-of-safety.md`.
Free cash flow is formed once, and the payout on it moves onto that module. The
value per share is a year's free cash flow grown over a forecast and a
perpetuity, less net debt, per diluted share. It has four settings, with presets
from Damodaran's January 2026 cost of capital and the FOMC's 2% goal. The margin
is the value less the valuation date's close, over the value. Five `declined`
conditions are added. Net interest counted twice, preferred and NCI left in,
and financial kinds computed are named with their direction. Two questions are
left open: net debt averaged under the period rule, and the share-basis rule's
use of the date ordering.
