# 2026-09-04 — M4-14

Branch `M4-14`. `docs/adr/period-alignment.md`, proposed: a filer's periods are
the ones its own facts date, every filing that reports a period answers it and
is resolved inside that one filing, and the value is the one the filing with the
greatest `filed` states. A fiscal-year change takes no clause, because nothing
labels a period; an amendment is a filing like any other, and `form` is read
once, to break a tie on `filed`. Code in `vfi-normalize`, no new data surface,
no field added to any published contract.

For the decider. The record proposes one field as a `fetch-normalize` v2 — the
filing's own period of report, EDGAR's `reportDate` — and does not write it. It
is load-bearing: without it, the cover-page share count hands
`shares_outstanding` at 2023-12-31 a figure from a cover page nineteen months
later. At v1 that entry is never a candidate, so the cost is an `Unknown` and
never a wrong number.

Every rule was checked against the merged company-facts fixture and the
submissions fixture for the same filer, and three of the seven alternatives are
rejected on what those fixtures show rather than on argument.
