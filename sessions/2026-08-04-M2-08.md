# 2026-08-04 — M2-08

Branch `M2-08`. A slow engine job that keeps its caller responsive.

New non-stage crate `vfi-jobs`: a job runs on its own thread, and the caller
holds a handle it can read, stop, and take the result from without waiting.
`slow_job` has no end of its own, which is what lets the test prove concurrency
with no sleeps and no timing assumptions. `docs/layout.md` records the crate,
and the root manifest gains its member line — the one edit outside `owns`,
without which the crate would neither build nor be tested.

Gates run with `--gates-only`; the proof-of-catch half is CI's on the pull
request, and no gate changed here. That is M2's last criterion.
