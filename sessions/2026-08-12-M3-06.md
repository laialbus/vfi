# 2026-08-12 — M3-06

Branch `M3-06`. `vfi-fetch` gains `ledger::Journal`: M3-05's interface over an
append-only file at a path the caller hands it. A line is a record's length, a
space, and that many bytes of JSON, written in one `write_all` with nothing
buffered above the file. The length is what makes a record cut short
recognisable anywhere in the file, so a killed process costs that record and no
other; the next append opens a line of its own rather than continuing the wreck.
Records are read out of the file, not out of anything kept beside it — what is
kept beside it is one offset per record, so a filer's verdicts are not a scan of
every verdict ever recorded.

The torn tail is proved rather than argued: a journal cut at every byte of the
record in flight, reopened at each, still holding what landed before it. A
record whose bytes all landed and lost only its terminator reads back, which is
the one place "at most the record in flight" resolves in the record's favour.

`Reason` and `Judged` now hold `Cow<'static, str>`. Writing one still costs a
`&'static str`, so a rule composed at the call site does not compile; reading one
costs a `String`, because a journal outlives the ruleset that wrote it. The
read-back constructors are visible inside `ledger` and nowhere else.

Tests write to a directory of their own under the OS temp directory and take it
away again. Nothing outside `src/ledger/` opens a journal, the torn-tail test
included, which is why it is a unit test and not one under `tests/`.
