# 2026-08-11 — M3-03

Branch `M3-03`. `vfi-fetch` answers a ticker with the filing history EDGAR
publishes for it. `filing_history` resolves the ticker through EDGAR's
`company_tickers.json` to the CIK its endpoints key on, then reads that filer's
submissions document and every overflow page it names — a history stopping at
the first page would be missing its oldest years and would look complete. Every
record carries the `Source` of the request that produced it, the company
included, and `Retrieved` names three outcomes: a ticker the map does not name,
a filer with nothing filed, and a history. All requests go out through M3-01's
chokepoint.

Three golden fixtures under `fixtures/fetch/`, recorded from live EDGAR and
committed as the bodies arrived. Each `expected` is written from the recording
rather than by running the stage, so the two readings are what agree. No test
reaches the network.

Two for the decider. `vfi-fetch` takes the workspace's first third-party
dependencies, serde and serde_json; the alternative was hand-rolling a JSON
reader for a source document. And the no-filings outcome has no recording
behind it — EDGAR appears to publish none, since a CIK reaches the submissions
endpoint by filing — so it is pinned by a document written in
`tests/history.rs`, with that caveat in the file's header.
