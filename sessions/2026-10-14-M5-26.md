# 2026-10-14 — M5-26

Branch `M5-26`. Proposes `docs/adr/which-other-period-a-metric-reads.md`. A
flow-over-balance metric is stated at the duration and reads the balance at
closing (equality on the end), opening (the start stepped back one day, per
XBRL 2.1 §4.7.2, through one RFC 3339 date reading in `date.rs`) or averaged.
Altman's terms read closing, Piotroski's opening or averaged, and standalone
returns and asset turnover the average (CFA). `sought_by` is `opening_instant`
or `closing_instant`; an unreadable start is `declined` as `start_not_a_date`.
The preceding duration and series are left to their own record.
