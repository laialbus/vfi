# 2026-09-07 — M4-16

Branch `M4-16`. `vfi-normalize` gains `applicability`, the question asked ahead
of every other: given a concept and the filer's kind, either the kind is
excluded — and the answer is `NotApplicable` carrying that kind and the clause
that excluded it — or the attempt proceeds. It reads the concept's own
applicability clause and nothing else: no registry is loaded, no filing is read,
no rule is looked up, and no period is taken, the state being a function of the
kind.

The exclusion is built the one way the compiled vocabulary allows, through
`Applicability::excluding`, which is also the only reading of a clause anywhere
in the diff — `admits` is not called in the module, and no clause is copied into
this crate. Three tests sweep the closed sets whole: every concept against every
kind with the expected answer read off the same published clause rather than
tabulated beside it, every concept against a filer with no kind, and the two put
side by side so a subject answering by concept alone fails. Nothing resolves;
`normalize` and the golden fixture are untouched.

For the decider. Two shapes worth a look. `Answer` has two members and not
three: whether the attempt then finds a value or finds nothing is the attempt's
to say, and a third member here would be a question that had run one. And
`Answer::Excluded` carries the whole `Resolution` rather than the kind and the
clause loose, so assembling a correct absence stays in one place.
