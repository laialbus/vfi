Branch `M4-35`. New `vfi_normalize::standing::stands`: one concept at one
period over every answering filing — applicability first, then the silence
reading over the period, then Rule 3. `SetBy::Silence` now keeps the attempt its
four steps found, and `filings::Attempted` carries `reached`, the one bit that
crosses between filings. Tests in `tests/standing.rs`: the nine hand-written
cases, plus both restatement tables for CIK 0002003750 and `preferred_equity` at
2024-12-31 for CIK 0001859199 against the merged fixtures. Every claim
re-derived. No existing test changed.

For the decider. Two things neither record states, handled in the crate's
existing direction: a proceeding concept at a period no filing answers is
`None`, not an `Unknown` with no attempt; and a `Value` from a filing whose
`filed` does not read as one date is `Unknown` (`Undecided::Undated`), not
ordered. Rule 3's tie is applied as written to silence zeros too, so two
same-day filings both silent would be `Unknown`. No merged fixture has that
shape.
