# 2026-10-07 — M5-16

Branch `M5-16`. Proposes `docs/adr/how-analyze-computes-and-writes-a-figure.md`.
Analyze reads an amount or close in normalize's decimal grammar as an exact
rational; anything outside it is `declined` as `input_not_a_decimal`. It
computes exactly, with only a k-th root rounded where taken. It writes every
figure and numeric setting once, to 34 significant digits, ties away, plain
positional. `RADIX` (RFC 8259) and `SIGNIFICANT_DIGITS` (IEEE 754-2008
decimal128) go in `constants/figure.rs`. No unit scale, no dependency.
