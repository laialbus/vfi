# 2026-09-08 — M4-17

Branch `M4-17`. `vfi-normalize` gains `answering`: given a concept, one eligible
rule, one period and the facts of one filing, which of those facts answer that
period — one reading per element operand, in the rule's own order, each holding
every fact that answers it. A flow takes only a duration and a balance only an
instant; the dates are compared for equality as the boundary publishes them,
with no day count and no window; a fact answers only in the unit the vocabulary
names for the concept. An operand nothing answered is in the answer as the
operand it is, carrying nothing. The entry that answers the filing it was
reported in takes its instant fact in that filing whatever period is asked.
Nothing ranks, nothing is dropped for its `reading`, a `difference`'s concept
operand is not in the answer at all, and no fifth rule form appears. `normalize`
and the golden fixture are untouched.

Eleven tests, every fact in them read out of `every-fact-a-filer-reported`
rather than written for the case, and every rule reached through the registry
interface — the committed registry where it has the entry, a copy plus one entry
where the refusal has to be planted because the mapping never states it. The
sweep asks every period the filer reports of every one of its filings and tallies
that each fact answered exactly once.

For the decider. One fork worth a look, taken in the module header. The record
says a balance's instant answers "when it is the period's end", which admits
reading a duration period at its end; this takes the strict half, so a balance is
answered at the instant it is asked at and not over a quarter. The alignment
ruleset makes every date a fact carries a period of its own, so nothing is
unreachable under the strict reading, and the loose one would put one figure
under two period keys. Also: what makes a unit key a currency is that it is three
uppercase letters, the shape ISO 4217 fixes — enough to keep `pure` and `sqm` out
of a concept measured in money, and no copy of ISO's list in this repository.
