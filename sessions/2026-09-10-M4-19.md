# 2026-09-10 — M4-19

Branch `M4-19`. `vfi-normalize` gains `filings`: given a filer's facts and one
period, which of that filer's filings answer it — a filing answers by carrying
one fact stated for that period, and by nothing else — and the candidate-choice
procedure run once inside each of them, over the facts carrying that filing's
accession and no other's. `docs/adr/period-alignment.md`'s Rule 2, whole.
Nothing ranks: the answering filings come back in accession order, an attempt
that returned nothing is in the list beside one that resolved, and `filed`,
`form` and the fact count are unread. `normalize`, the golden fixture, the
contracts and the registry data are untouched.

Seven cases over `every-fact-a-filer-reported`. The quarter 2024-10-01 to
2024-12-31 is answered by six filings, two carrying 40 and 37 facts at it and
four carrying two apiece; `net_income` resolves in all six and `revenue` in the
two that report it, so reading the newest filing alone would lose four of five
statements for a quarter the filer published in full. The quarter 2023-10-01 to
2023-12-31 is answered by five filings, none of them its own report, the fullest
filed 2025-02-14. Every fact behind every value carries the accession of the
filing its attempt ran inside, checked across all 28 concepts at three periods.

For the decider. Three judgements worth a look.

The cover-page entry is refused by a new argument on the door rather than by a
rule inside it. `answering::Admits` says which of the registry's entries a filing
is asked with, and `filings` passes `OnlyThePeriodAskedFor` because the condition
the record sets — the filing's own period of report ending on the period asked
for — names a field `fetch-normalize` v1 does not carry, so it is never met. The
other variant is what v2 will pass and what M4-17 and M4-18's cases pass, which
is why what those two answer about one filing is unchanged and no test of theirs
was rewritten. Refused, the entry declines as "no fact answering the period asked
for", so the closed set of six decline reasons stays closed.

`shares_outstanding` at the instant 2023-12-31 therefore has no candidate at all
in any of the five filings that answer it, and the case measures what the other
reading would have cost on the same fixture: 60,500,000 off a cover page nineteen
months later, against the filer's own 60,000,000 at the quarter three months on.

`settle` and `ask` now take `&[&Fact]` rather than `&[Fact]`. A filing's facts
are a selection out of the filer's, and the state a filing's attempt returns
borrows them, so the alternative was copying every fact of every answering filing
per period to give the borrow something to point at.
