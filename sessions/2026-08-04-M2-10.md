# 2026-08-04 — M2-10

Branch `M2-10`. `scripts/tasks.sh` splits the sweep's single remedy in two. A
claim sitting at a commit main already has is deleted as before; a claim holding
commits main does not have is moved to `escalated/<id>-swept-<last-seen>` in the
same atomic push that releases it, and origin is read back to prove the archive
holds what the claim held. The age rule and the open-pull-request rule are
untouched.

The corpus pins both outcomes through the `release <id> <commit> <seen>`
subcommand rather than through a sweep, because a sweep decides from origin's
activity record and from its pull requests, and neither is the scratch
repository's to answer for.
