# 2026-07-31 — M1-06

Branch: `M1-06`

`tasks.sh available` now refuses the whole listing at exit 3 when a frontmatter
line spells `id`, `depends_on`, or `exclusive` in a way the parser would not
read as that key — different case, a space before the colon, or an indent.
Previously such a line was silently ignored, so an exclusive task was offered
as ordinary and a task could be offered before its dependencies merged.

The key must be the whole text before the colon, after case and surrounding
whitespace are stripped, so `exclusive_reason` and prose that quotes or bullets
a key inside a value are untouched. Verified against the previous script over a
corpus of well-formed, variant, and prose files: identical on every well-formed
and unrelated-key case, refusing on every variant.
