# 2026-08-08 — M3-01

Branch `M3-01`. Everything `vfi-fetch` sends now leaves through one chokepoint:
`Egress::fetch` reads the host out of the URL, checks it against
`ALLOWED_HOSTS`, and only then hands a `Cleared` to a transport. Nothing outside
`crates/fetch/src/egress/` can construct a `Cleared`, so a request the list has
not seen does not compile. An unlisted host comes back as `Error::Withheld`
naming it, with nothing sent, and a URL two readers would disagree about —
userinfo, a port, a scheme that is not exactly https — is refused rather than
resolved.

`gates.sh` gains an `egress` gate for the half privacy cannot hold: the names
that open a connection may appear in the chokepoint and nowhere else in the
workspace. Its proof plants the same call site inside the chokepoint, where it
must stay green, and in another crate, where it must go red. Nothing added here
reaches the network.
