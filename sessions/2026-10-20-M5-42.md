# 2026-10-20 — M5-42

Branch `M5-42`. Proposes `docs/adr/dividend-growth-rate.md`. The rate is the
compound rate over `dividend_growth_years`, reading the computing year and the
base year, finding the years between and reading none. An excess between moves
nothing; at the computing year it overstates, at the base year it understates,
and the split that would fix either does not cross v2. A zero base year is the
new `zero_base_year_dividends_declared_per_share`; a zero computing year is
`-1`. Damodaran's *Investment Valuation* ch. 11 was read from the author's copy.
