# 2026-10-15 — M5-31

Branch `M5-31`. Proposes `docs/adr/capital-employed-invested-capital-and-nopat.md`.
Capital employed is total assets less current liabilities; invested capital is
shareholders' equity plus net debt, cash and short-term investments netted;
NOPAT is operating income after the effective rate. All are formed once in one
module, which also forms total and net debt for `debt_to_equity` and enterprise
value. The rate declines at a non-positive pretax income and outside zero to
one, never clamped and with no setting. Five new consumers are named within the
record's authority. Non-controlling interests and mezzanine preferred are left
open for a v3. A negative capital gives a figure, flagged for the decider.
